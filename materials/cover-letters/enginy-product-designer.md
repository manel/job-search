Hello Enginy team,

This role caught my attention because it describes the way I like to work: close to engineers, making things real early, and staying with a problem after it ships.

At Remuner, a B2B sales compensation platform, I was the sole designer across the product. I worked directly with the founders and engineers, without a product manager. One of the products I designed was an AI agent for questions about commissions and sales plans. I owned its conversation design, answer formats, capability boundaries, and trust patterns, and it shipped to every customer.

The maker part of the role also feels familiar. Earlier in my career I built CMS-connected front ends and reusable component libraries. More recently, I used Codex to extend Remuner's token pipeline with CI checks, drift detection, smoke tests, an HTML preview, and additional outputs. The work went through PRs reviewed and merged by engineering.

Before Remuner, I designed a self-serve data-management layer at Abacum that turned JSON configuration into a guided interface for finance teams. Enginy brings together several things I already know well: sales software, AI behavior, dense workflows, and small teams where design works directly with engineering. That is what makes the role interesting to me.

The closest examples are manel-leiva.com/cases/ai-agent and manel-leiva.com/cases/design-system-remuner. I would be happy to walk you through them.
