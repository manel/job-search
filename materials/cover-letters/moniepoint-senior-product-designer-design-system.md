Hello Moniepoint team,

This role caught my attention because it treats a design system as product infrastructure: components, tokens, governance, adoption, and the connection to production code.

At Abacum, an FP&A platform, I co-led the business case for a design system spanning five product squads. I worked on the two-tier token architecture, component library, and pipeline from Figma through Style Dictionary to the frontend. I also ran the weekly library sync and contribution process, while squads migrated existing product surfaces through their own backlogs.

At Remuner, a sales compensation platform, I was the sole designer and built the component library and token system from scratch alongside regular product work. I ran an ongoing audit with engineering to define missing states and variants and decide when each improvement should ship. I later used Codex to add CI validation, drift detection, smoke tests, an HTML preview, and additional token outputs through PRs reviewed and merged by engineers.

The two systems were built under different constraints, but the recurring problem was the same: make design decisions reusable, keep Figma and code aligned, and create a governance model the team can sustain.
