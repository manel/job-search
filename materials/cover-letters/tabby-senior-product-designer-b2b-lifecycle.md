Hello Tabby team,

What interests me about the B2B Lifecycle role is helping partners get through a complicated setup and understand what they can do next. Much of my recent work has involved making complex financial software usable by the people who need it every day.

At Abacum, I led design and customer testing for the Dataset Manager, replacing exposed JSON configuration with a guided interface for finance teams. Testing showed that part of the problem was the language: users thought in dimensions, mappings, and column formats, while the product spoke in technical data types. Translating that logic into something they could work with helped customers reach a working configuration in their first session.

More recently, I was the sole designer at Remuner, a B2B sales-compensation platform. I worked directly with founders and engineers across the product, built and maintained its design system, and ran an ongoing audit to make patterns and states consistent as we shipped. I also designed and launched an AI agent for questions about commissions and sales plans.

I'd like to bring that experience to Tabby's partner onboarding and activation flows. The focus on clear feedback, understandable language, and helping partners complete tasks independently is a close match for the work I enjoy.
