Hello AirHelp team,

This role caught my attention because it connects several parts of a B2B experience that are often designed separately: the product a partner uses, the journey their customer sees, and the material that helps the partner understand and launch it.

At Abacum, I led design and customer testing for the Dataset Manager, a guided interface over JSON-based configuration. Finance teams could manage their own data instead of depending on implementation support for routine changes. The implementation team's read was that standard configuration took roughly half the previous time, and customers could reach a working setup in their first session.

More recently, I was the sole designer at Remuner, a B2B sales-compensation platform. I owned design across the product and worked directly with founders and engineers. I designed and shipped an AI agent for questions about commissions, sales plans, and team information, and ran an ongoing product audit to make patterns, states, and components more consistent across the experience.

I have not worked in travel tech, but the underlying design problems are familiar: complex business rules, different user contexts, sensitive financial information, and the need to turn one product story into a coherent experience. I am also interested in the broader scope of this role, where research and product design continue into partner communication and launch material rather than stopping at the interface.
