Hello Alan team,

This role caught my attention because it combines two kinds of design work I enjoy: helping a team shape a product from an uncertain starting point, and making complicated systems feel clear to the people using them. Alan Clinic and the work to build a health network for Spain make that challenge especially concrete.

At Abacum, I led design and customer testing for the Dataset Manager. Finance teams depended on implementation specialists to configure their data through JSON. An early direction added a simpler interface and SQL, but testing showed that users could express the same logic in familiar spreadsheet terms and not in SQL. We changed direction and built guided workflows around concepts they already understood.

More recently, I was the sole designer at Remuner, a sales-compensation platform. I worked directly with founders and engineers across the product and designed an AI agent for questions about commissions and sales plans. I owned its UX/UI, conversation design, answer formats, capability boundaries, and trust patterns. It shipped to every customer.

I have not worked in healthcare, but the underlying challenge is familiar: complex rules, sensitive information, different user contexts, and a need for the interface to make the next step understandable. I would like to bring that experience to Alan and work closely with product, research, and engineering on products people rely on in important moments.
