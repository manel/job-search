Hello CloudTalk team,

This role caught my attention because it brings together three parts of my recent work: complex B2B products, design systems, and AI experiences that need to earn users' trust.

At Remuner, a sales-compensation platform, I was the sole designer across the product and worked directly with founders and engineers. I built and maintained its component library and token system, and designed an AI agent that answered questions about commissions and sales plans. I owned the conversation design, answer formats, capability boundaries, and trust patterns. The agent shipped to every customer, and we reviewed questions, answers, and user feedback each week to improve how it responded.

Before that, I helped build and run Abacum's design system across five product squads. I worked on its component library, token architecture, governance, migration process, and pipeline from Figma through Style Dictionary to the frontend. I also led design and customer testing for a self-serve data-management product, translating technical configuration into workflows finance teams could understand and operate safely.

CloudTalk's combination of AI-powered communication, product work across different surfaces, and design-system maturity is close to the problems I enjoy working on. I would be interested in helping make the calling experience clear and dependable while keeping shared patterns and implementation aligned as the product grows.
