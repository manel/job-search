What to do with this

  1. Nudge at day 10 to 14, then stop counting on it. Silence after two weeks rarely turns into an interview.
  2. Apply in a posting's first days. Use the scanner's new-postings list.
  3. No cold applications to DS-titled roles until the DS CV review is done. Use referrals or Product Designer roles with system scope.
  4. Put more time into interview prep than into application volume.

Small numbers: read percentages as direction, not rates. Channel and lane are not recorded for about half the pre-September applications. Biorce and Factorial use the screen date, so their wait is an upper bound.
