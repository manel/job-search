# Interview feedback: weaknesses and recommendations (2026-11-18)

## Summary

You explain well what you did. You do not show enough what it changed or where it should go next.

The CV screen is no longer the main problem. All 10 conversations that have ended were rejections, with 0 offers. The [2026-11-03 funnel analysis](../2026-11-03/response-times.md) counted 8. Enginy and Perk add 2.

## Evidence limits

- Five companies gave specific feedback: Factorial, Spendesk, Alan, Wizeline and Perk.
- Spendesk's feedback is your own report of a phone call.
- Datavant and Enginy gave no specific reason. Enginy has not answered the feedback request of 2026-11-14.
- Template rejections say nothing about the cause.
- Items marked **Inference** are my interpretation. No employer stated them.

## Weaknesses

### 1. Impact stays general (strongest pattern)

| Company | Feedback |
|---|---|
| [Factorial](../../interviews/factorial/factorial.md) | Wanted "impact thinking" and "a clear proposition beyond what a manual workflow already solves" |
| [Alan, first interview](../../applications/records/alan-product-designer.md) | Answers about impact and strategy stayed general |
| [Perk](../../interviews/perk/perk.md) | Wanted adoption tied to user and business value |
| [Wizeline](../../interviews/wizeline/wizeline-stage2-case.md) | The case did not show what you bring to the table |

### 2. You do not lead enough in live exercises

- Factorial wanted a designer who can "independently drive" the shift from validation to vision.
- Alan said discovery and anticipating user challenges "required significant guidance". It also named proactive simplification and time management.
- In [the Alan case](../../interviews/alan/alan-technical-interview.md), the interviewer steered you back to matching. Your metric was the number of consultations, with no target.
- Your synthesis moved away from the given data point in two mocks and in the Alan case ([memory.md](../../memory.md), 2026-10-16).

### 3. Answers weaken outside your prep

All of these Perk questions were marked "not in prep":

- Adapting the approach for 40 designers: the answer did not finish.
- Choosing 16, 12 or 8 px padding: the answer had no principle.
- Organising docs from one source: "I don't really know".
- Showing a component you elevated visually: "Not really".

On enforcement, you had mechanisms in the register, but you did not connect them to the question.

### 4. Facts slip under pressure

No employer named this. It is a risk with a careful interviewer.

- **Perk:** 45% instead of 47%; July 2024 instead of July 2022; the wrong timing for the Storybook template; 8 of 18 instead of 10 of 18.
- **Mapon video:** two Maker Days periods mixed; 5 components instead of 7; an implied time saving that nobody measured.

### 5. No visual craft piece to show

Perk named this gap first. Only Perk has raised it directly, but it blocks any role with a visual mandate.

## What you are doing wrong

- **You treat each rejection as a separate event.** The Factorial notes say "a failed live challenge, not a career signal". Weaknesses 1 and 2 now repeat across companies.
- **You may have overcorrected after Factorial (Inference).** You moved to vision first. At Alan, you framed the problem from the vision paragraph and did not start from the drop-off data.
- **Your preparation favours stories and decks.** Most of the damage comes from follow-up questions and live reasoning.
- **Your read after interviews is too optimistic.** You felt Alan and Perk went well. Our Perk assessment, "likely enough to advance", was also wrong.
- **You still apply to roles with a known gap (Inference).** The Knot, AIOS and Finom rejected you at the screen. None gave a reason.

## Less current

Spendesk's "very low presentation skills" looks less current. Wizeline called your presentation good, and Perk did not raise it. Neither company measured it directly, so do not treat it as closed.

## Recommendations, in order

1. **Add an impact line to every story.** State what changed for users or the business. Where no measure exists, say "we did not measure it".
2. **Practise live cases in a fixed order.** Given data point, problem, one flow, metric with baseline and target, then vision.
3. **Practise unprepared follow-up questions aloud.** Answer each with a principle first and an example second.
4. **Finish the visual craft piece.** It is already the first item in [TODO.md](../../TODO.md).

The dated plan is in [interview-fix-plan.md](interview-fix-plan.md).
