# Compensation strategy

## Evidence boundary

No recorded employer has explicitly said that compensation caused a rejection. Generic rejection emails cannot establish that conclusion.

Manel has nevertheless observed a repeated signal in recruiter conversations: except where some job descriptions published higher ranges, the ranges discussed for Spain generally did not exceed EUR 60,000-65,000. He also recalls Secfix describing EUR 67,000-70,000 as somewhat high for Spain.

Treat this as credible first-hand recollection about market budgets, not as a measured market-wide salary distribution.

## Decision rule

1. Prefer roles with a published range.
2. When no range is published, ask for it before giving a number whenever the process allows.
3. If a form forces a number, calibrate it to the verified level and company context rather than applying one figure to every Senior role.
4. Do not invest heavily in a process whose likely ceiling is below Manel's walk-away floor.
5. Record four separate facts: employer range, whether salary was asked, what Manel stated, and whether the employer gave compensation feedback.
6. Never label salary as a rejection reason unless the employer says so.
7. Match the form. If it accepts a range, a range is fine; if it asks for one number, give one number, not a range.
8. Never share current or previous salary. If asked: "I'm not sharing previous compensation, but I'm targeting [X] for this role based on scope and market data."

## Current Senior position

- **Priority update, confirmed by Manel 2026-10-11:** Income continuity now takes precedence over previous compensation expectations. Manel is willing to accept lower pay for viable work. The previous approximate EUR 60,000 floor is no longer an automatic rejection gate; a replacement numeric minimum is unknown. Assess actual pay, hours and contract terms when available. Do not interpret flexibility as willingness to accept any amount or volunteer personal financial pressure to employers.
- **Floor stated by Manel 2026-10-20 (Wizeline discussion):** EUR 40,000 gross annual is acceptable for a full-time employment contract in Barcelona; 40-48K "I can do", with starting paid work as the main concern. Treat 40K as the walk-away floor for employed full-time roles until Manel revises it. Freelance or agency contracts need the equivalent employed value (roughly 25-30% higher headline). Never state the floor to an employer; open higher (for Wizeline, around 50K).
- Previous working minimum for a Senior Product Designer role: approximately EUR 60,000 gross annual base (superseded as a hard gate by the update above).
- Preference: maximize compensation above that floor when the role, company, and published range support it.
- EUR 60,000 is a historical comparator, not the default opening number or current hard floor.
- When a range is published, use the range and role fit to choose the position; do not automatically volunteer the floor.
- Total compensation, role quality, flexibility, and growth may affect the final decision, so treat the floor as approximate until evaluating an actual offer.
