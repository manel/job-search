# Compensation strategy

## Evidence boundary

No recorded employer has explicitly said that compensation caused a rejection. Generic rejection emails cannot establish that conclusion.

Manel has nevertheless observed a repeated signal in recruiter conversations: except where some job descriptions published higher ranges, the ranges discussed for Spain generally did not exceed EUR 60,000-65,000. He also recalls Secfix describing EUR 67,000-70,000 as somewhat high for Spain.

Treat this as credible first-hand recollection about market budgets, not as a measured market-wide salary distribution.

## Decision rule

1. Prefer roles with a published range.
2. When no range is published, ask for it before giving a number whenever the process allows.
3. If a form forces a number, calibrate it to the verified level and company context rather than applying one figure to every Senior role.
4. Do not invest heavily in a process whose likely ceiling is below Manel's walk-away floor.
5. Record four separate facts: employer range, whether salary was asked, what Manel stated, and whether the employer gave compensation feedback.
6. Never label salary as a rejection reason unless the employer says so.
