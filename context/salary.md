# Salary

## Rules (never break these)

- Always open at the top of the range.
- Never give a range when the ceiling is known. State the number.
- Never share previous salary if asked. Redirect: "I'm targeting X based on the scope of the role and market data."
- Compensation was below market for scope at previous roles. Do not anchor to past numbers.
- Barcelona Senior Product Designer 75th percentile benchmark: ~€93K (Levels.fyi data).

## What to do when a role doesn't have an agreed position

1. Assess the scope: Senior / Lead / Staff IC?
2. Reference the €93K benchmark for Senior at 75th percentile in Barcelona.
3. Adjust up for Staff or Lead scope, adjust for remote premium if applicable.
4. Set the position before writing the cover letter. Don't leave it open.

## If asked for current or previous salary

Do not answer directly. Use: "I'm not sharing previous compensation, but I'm targeting [X] for this role based on scope and market data."
