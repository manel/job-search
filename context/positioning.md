# Positioning

## Core position

Senior Product Designer based in Barcelona. Strongest in complex B2B workflows, data-heavy products, product architecture, design systems, and close product-engineering collaboration.

The useful through-line is making complex product logic understandable and usable, not visual-production work alone.

## Role breadth

- **Remuner:** Sole designer with product-design responsibility across the sales-compensation platform, including data integrations and configuration, compensation plans and payouts, rep and manager experiences, approval workflows, roles and permissions, and administration. AI-agent and design-system work are selected examples within that remit.
- **Abacum:** Product-design work across the Integrations squad and later the Engine squad, covering integrations, dataset workflows, dimensions, mappings, models, and the modeling language. Dataset Manager is one selected project, not the whole role.

Lead each CV role with this scope, then choose the evidence most relevant to the vacancy. Product-design ownership does not imply sole product-strategy or engineering ownership.

## Proof themes

- Product architecture and self-serve configuration, especially Dataset Manager and formula-language work.
- Two production design systems, including design-token and implementation-pipeline work.
- Shipped AI product work, including conversation design, capability boundaries, answer formats, and trust patterns.

## Target hiring manager

Head of Design or VP Design at a B2B SaaS or fintech company, Series A-C or established. They have a design-systems gap or a complex product area that needs a senior IC who can work independently and cross-functionally. They are not hiring someone to manage a team. Under the current income-first priority (`context/target-roles.md`), this is the strongest-fit profile, not a filter.
