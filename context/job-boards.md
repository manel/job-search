# Job Boards

Where to search, what works, and what doesn't. Updated via /remember when new signals emerge.

---

## Weekly sourcing ritual

Once a week, same day every week (Monday recommended — postings cluster Mon/Tue and early applications get read):

1. Browse, in order: LinkedIn (filtered search below), Manfred, Welcome to the Jungle, then direct careers pages for known targets (Linear, Factorial, plus any warm-path company).
2. Collect every plausible role as a URL or pasted JD. Don't self-filter beyond the obvious — the fit framework does the filtering, and pre-filtering by gut is how marginal-but-real fits get missed.
3. Run `/source-roles` with everything collected. It checks overdue follow-ups first, batch-assesses the new roles, and updates the pipeline.
4. Target: 3–5 roles assessed per week. If two consecutive weeks produce fewer than 2 plausible roles, add a secondary board to the rotation and say so here.

The discipline is the point. Sourcing only when the pipeline feels empty means sourcing three weeks too late: applications take 2–5 weeks to produce a response, so a thin week now is a silent month later.

## Primary boards

**LinkedIn**
Best signal-to-noise for Senior IC roles at B2B SaaS and fintech. Filter by: Barcelona or Remote, Senior / Lead / Staff. Check 2-3x per week. Most target companies post here first.

**Manfred**
Spanish tech job board. Good coverage of Barcelona-based and remote-friendly companies. Skewed toward Spanish market but international companies list here too. Worth checking weekly.

**Welcome to the Jungle**
Good for Series A-C startups. Company profiles give useful culture signal before applying. Filter aggressively — volume is high but relevance drops off fast outside product and design roles.

---

## Secondary boards

**Tecnoempleo**
Spanish market focus. Lower signal for Senior product design roles. Check occasionally rather than routinely.

**JobFluent**
Remote-first roles, Spanish market. Inconsistent coverage for design. Check when pipeline is thin.

---

## Direct company careers pages

For target companies, check careers pages directly rather than relying on boards. Boards lag by days and sometimes miss roles entirely.

Known targets to check manually:
- Linear: linear.app/careers
- Factorial: careers.factorialhr.com (proprietary ATS, boards unreliable)
- Any company identified through warm paths

---

## Limitations to know

- Manfred, Tecnoempleo, JobFluent, and Welcome to the Jungle are incompatible with automated scanning tools (career-ops). Check manually.
- Factorial's ATS does not surface reliably on any third-party board. Always go direct.
- LinkedIn Easy Apply roles tend to have higher applicant volume. For target companies, always apply via their own ATS if available.

---

## What has produced signal

- LinkedIn: primary source for roles assessed so far (BVNK, Typeform, Finom, Hostaway, Bitpanda)
- Direct company pages: Factorial (found via direct check)
- Warm paths: higher conversion than any board — prioritise over cold applications

## What hasn't

- career-ops automated scanning: results were low quality and low volume for Senior IC design roles. Discontinued.
