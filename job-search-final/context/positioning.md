# Positioning

## Core identity

Senior Product Designer with a systems thinking orientation. The work that defines the positioning is not visual craft or UI execution. It's: identifying structural problems in complex products, designing the architecture that solves them, and building the organizational alignment to ship that work.

Two production design systems built from scratch. Complex B2B workflow design across FP&A and sales compensation. A track record of working on problems most designers don't get close to: token pipelines, formula syntax, data abstraction layers.

## Differentiators

These are the things that are genuinely hard to find in a single candidate at Senior level:

1. **Two production design systems, end to end.** Not "contributed to." Built the business case, got engineering buy-in, implemented the token architecture, shipped to production. At Abacum (Abax, MUI-based, five squads) and Remuner (from scratch, smaller token set by design).

2. **Token pipeline ownership.** Token Studio to Style Dictionary to npm, with CI validation, drift checks, smoke tests, W3C migration, and HTML sandbox preview. Wrote the briefs, Codex implemented step by step. Goal was pipeline autonomy without engineering dependency.

3. **Product reasoning at the system level.** The Dataset Manager and Modeling Syntax cases both show the same thing: identifying that the real problem is upstream of the obvious one, then designing the right abstraction instead of the obvious feature.

4. **Shipped AI product work.** AI agent for sales reps and managers at Remuner. Not a concept or a prototype.

5. **Teaching background as a signal.** Taught UX and digital design at postgraduate level. Evidence of: explaining complex systems clearly, depth of craft, mentoring. Framed as skills developed, not a job title.

## Target persona (hiring manager)

Head of Design or VP Design at a B2B SaaS or fintech company, Series A–C or established. They have a design systems gap or a complex product area that needs a senior IC who can work independently. They are not looking for someone to manage a team. They are looking for someone who can own a hard problem and work cross-functionally to solve it.

## What I'm not

- Not a visual design generalist
- Not a UI production designer
- Not a design manager
- Not interested in consumer products or simple CRUD applications
- Not relocating

## Roles to skip

- Any role where the primary output is polished UI at speed
- Any role described primarily in terms of Figma component production
- Consumer-facing products without system complexity
- Management roles at any level
- Roles outside remote / Barcelona hybrid

## How to position for specific role types

**DS-first roles (BVNK, Hostaway, Bitpanda DS):**
Lead with Remuner DS work. Token pipeline is the headline technical signal. CTO quote is the credibility anchor. Abacum DS work is supporting evidence for scale.

**Product design roles with DS component (Typeform, Finom):**
Lead with Abacum integrations work (Dataset Manager, Modeling Syntax). Frame DS as a capability you bring to product work, not the other way around.

**Fintech / trading roles (Bitpanda Advanced Trading):**
Lead with Modeling Syntax. Domain knowledge: Kraken DCA user, familiar with trading mechanics. Not an expert, but not starting from zero.

**Founding designer roles (Factorial):**
Lead with the breadth: two companies, two systems, solo designer experience, ability to move from zero to production without infrastructure support.
