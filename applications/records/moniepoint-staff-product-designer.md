# Moniepoint — Staff Product Designer

## Decision

**Skip this specific posting.** The domain and location fit, but the advertised level is not credible and the work is less aligned than Moniepoint's separate Senior Product Designer - Design System vacancy. Do not draft materials or submit this application unless new information establishes genuinely Staff-level scope.

## Fit assessment

### Strong matches

- **Domain:** Moniepoint's financial products overlap with Manel's confirmed work on complex finance and compensation workflows at Abacum and Remuner.
- **Core product work:** The role includes end-to-end design, research, usability testing, product-engineering collaboration, complex financial concepts, and design-system contribution. These match the strongest parts of Manel's positioning.
- **Location:** The official posting is open for remote work in Spain.
- **IC scope:** The posting requires mentoring but states no direct-report or people-management accountability.

### Main risks and gaps

- **Level mismatch:** The official title is Staff Product Designer, but the body calls the position Senior UX/UI Designer and asks for only three or more years in a senior design role. LinkedIn classifies it as mid-senior. Moniepoint separately lists a Senior Product Designer (UX) role with essentially the same responsibilities and a five-year senior-experience requirement. This posting therefore does not establish distinct Staff-level scope, cross-team ownership, or organisational influence.
- **Work-shape mismatch:** The requirements put substantial weight on visual execution, mobile and web craft, behavioural psychology, motion tools such as Lottie and After Effects, and analytics or experimentation tooling. Manel's strongest differentiated evidence is complex B2B architecture, data-heavy workflows, and production design systems, not motion-led or consumer-mobile craft.
- **Outcome-evidence gap:** The application explicitly asks for measurable UX, business, or engagement impact. Manel has claimable operational signals and reconstructed measurements, but limited instrumented product metrics. Do not manufacture stronger outcomes.
- **Compensation:** No range is published. The Senior and Staff compensation targets should not be assumed to be equivalent, and the Staff target remains unconfirmed.
- **Posting quality:** The company description is visibly truncated in both the official posting and LinkedIn copy. Combined with the title/body mismatch and duplicated Senior vacancy, this suggests weak posting hygiene and increases calibration uncertainty.

## Level read

The responsibilities read as a broad **Senior UX/UI/Product Designer** role, not a Staff IC role. Mentoring, design strategy, stakeholder influence, and end-to-end ownership are compatible with Senior scope. The posting does not describe the multi-team product direction, organisation-level systems ownership, or sustained strategic leverage normally needed to justify Staff calibration.

## Proof case

If new evidence changes the decision, lead with [Data management layer, Abacum](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/dataset-manager). It is the closest evidence for the role's central problem: translating complex financial configuration into a usable self-serve product through research and close engineering collaboration.

Support it with [Design system from scratch, Remuner](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/design-system-remuner) for scalable components, tokens, and implementation partnership. Both cases were confirmed live on 2026-09-15.

## Better Moniepoint route

Moniepoint's official board also lists a **Senior Product Designer - Design System** role for remote Spain. That vacancy explicitly covers production-ready design systems, component architecture, design tokens, governance, design-engineering workflows, and cross-product scaling. It is materially closer to Manel's Remuner and Abacum evidence than this generic UX/UI posting and should be assessed separately before any Moniepoint materials are drafted.

No prior Moniepoint application or known warm connection is recorded. The only confirmed route is direct application through Moniepoint's Greenhouse board.
