# New Relic — Principal Product Designer

## Decision

**Proceed as a credible stretch, at medium-high priority.** The work is unusually well aligned with Manel's strongest evidence: complex B2B and data-heavy products, a shipped conversational AI agent, two production design systems, technical product architecture, documentation, and influence without formal authority. The role is a Barcelona-based individual-contributor position and has no people-management requirement.

This is not a clean Principal-level match. New Relic describes the role as its most senior individual design voice for the UX Platform, the Design Director's trusted deputy, and a quality bar across the whole product and design team. Manel has credible adjacent evidence, but the current record does not establish equivalent organization-wide authority, repeated Principal-level design judgment across a broad platform, or a sustained record of mentoring other designers. Apply as a stretch; do not rewrite Senior titles or imply formal Principal scope.

## Fit assessment

### Strong matches

- **Complex technical B2B products:** Dataset Manager translated JSON-based data configuration into a self-serve interface for finance teams. The modeling-language work joined research, system architecture, and engineering constraints. This maps well to a platform used by technical customers, even though observability and developer tooling are new domains.
- **AI-native product experience:** Manel designed and shipped Remuner's conversational AI agent to all customers. He owned the interface, conversation design, answer formats, design-side capability boundaries, refusal behavior, and trust patterns. Post-release review covered unpredictable outputs, incorrect answers, comprehension problems, and data freshness. This is direct evidence for non-deterministic AI UX, with no claimable adoption, accuracy, or revenue metric.
- **Design-system stewardship:** At Abacum, Manel co-led the business case with the Head of Design and worked across five squads on token architecture, components, governance, migration, and the Figma-to-code pipeline. At Remuner, he built the system from zero as sole designer and ran its governance with Engineering.
- **Influence without authority:** Abax won dedicated engineering time twice; Manel presented the case to engineering leadership, ran an eight-month cross-design governance cadence, and established contribution, issue-reporting, migration, naming, and deprecation processes.
- **Independent ownership and ambiguity:** Manel was the first designer on the Dataset Manager squad and later the sole designer at Remuner. The evidence supports framing ambiguous problems, testing directions, working through technical constraints, and driving work to release.
- **AI-assisted workflows and traceability:** Manel used Codex to harden the Remuner token repository with CI validation, drift checks, smoke tests, previews, and additional outputs; engineers reviewed and merged the changes. This supports hands-on AI-assisted work and documented, reviewable workflows, not sole engineering authorship or proof that he has already defined an organization-wide spec-driven design practice.

### Risks and gaps

- **Principal calibration is the main risk:** The vacancy expects the most senior design judgment below the Design Director, strategic direction across the full UX Platform, and authority to deputize in Product and Engineering reviews. The evidence supports senior cross-functional influence and systems leadership, but not this exact breadth or formal standing.
- **Mentoring and team-wide craft leadership:** The role explicitly asks the hire to mentor designers and raise the team's craft level. Cross-team governance and shared design-system practice are supported; direct mentoring, critique leadership, or designer-development outcomes are not recorded.
- **Observability and developer tools:** These are bonus qualifications, not hard requirements. No direct observability-product experience is recorded. Technical B2B data products and design-to-code infrastructure are adjacent evidence, not substitutes to present as domain experience.
- **Product-wide quality outcomes:** Abax adoption and Remuner system coverage provide concrete operating evidence, but the record does not show repeated responsibility for quality across an entire large product organization.
- **AI impact evidence:** The AI agent shipped to all customers and had an evidence-review loop, but there are no claimable adoption, accuracy, satisfaction, or business-impact metrics.
- **Communication bar:** Excellent written and verbal English is required. The repository contains English application and portfolio work, but the evidence register does not explicitly confirm spoken-English proficiency. Confirm if the application asks.
- **Compensation and working pattern:** The job description gives no salary range or specific office cadence. The flexible-workforce statement allows office, remote, or hybrid models generally, but does not guarantee which arrangement applies to this Barcelona position.

## Level read

The responsibilities substantiate the Principal title. This is not a Senior role with an inflated label: New Relic wants a company-level multiplier who can act without oversight, shape strategy, stand in for a director, and improve other designers' work. Manel's experience is sufficient to make the application credible, but the strongest evidence sits at Senior-to-Lead/Staff scope. Treat selection as dependent on whether New Relic values demonstrated systems building and AI product work over prior formal Principal scope.
