# DuckDuckGo - Senior Product Designer, Design Systems

## Decision

**Proceed as a medium-priority stretch.** The role is a genuine Senior design-systems IC position with unusually direct matches on system ownership, tokens and libraries, design-engineering workflows, and AI-assisted maintenance. The published compensation is materially above the current Senior floor, and the application form does not add a binary consumer-brand or native-platform gate.

This is not a clean fit. DuckDuckGo explicitly asks for at least two years in senior design positions at significant global consumer brands and wants a system that spans consumer products on web, macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android. Manel's strongest confirmed evidence is B2B SaaS and web design systems. Apply as a stretch; do not imply global-consumer-brand or native-platform system experience.

## Fit assessment

### Strong matches

- **Dedicated system ownership:** This is DuckDuckGo's first role of its kind. The hire will audit the current system, define its roadmap, prioritise quick wins and larger projects, and establish how it scales. Manel built Remuner's system from zero as sole designer and co-led the Abax business case, architecture, governance, migration, and engineering pipeline across five squads.
- **Design-to-code practice:** The role explicitly asks for shared nomenclature and workflows across design and engineering. Abax and Remuner provide direct evidence across Figma libraries and variables, token architecture, Storybook, contribution rules, implementation review, and engineering-reviewed repository work. Do not claim sole technical authorship of the original token pipeline.
- **AI-assisted system work:** DuckDuckGo specifically requires agentic and AI-driven approaches to system management. At Remuner, Manel used Codex to add CI validation, drift detection, smoke tests, previews, W3C-format tokens, and implementation outputs through engineer-reviewed changes. The shipped AI agent adds product-level AI experience, although it is supporting rather than central proof for this role.
- **Ambiguous, autonomous scope:** Manel was the sole designer at Remuner and the first designer on Abacum's Dataset Manager squad. The record supports independently framing problems, proposing directions, documenting decisions, and carrying work through implementation with Product and Engineering.
- **Experience threshold:** Current approved CV copy states 15 years in design, including formal Senior Product Designer roles from January 2022 to March 2026. This clears the numeric threshold without satisfying the separate consumer-brand requirement.
- **IC scope:** The posting describes autonomous project leadership and cross-functional influence, with no direct reports or people-management accountability.

### Main risks and gaps

- **Global consumer-brand experience:** This is the clearest mismatch. Abacum and Remuner are B2B software companies, not significant global consumer brands. Earlier studio clients do not establish two years in a senior in-house position at such a brand.
- **Native and multi-platform systems:** DuckDuckGo operates across macOS, Windows, iOS, Android, Search, and Duck.ai. Confirmed design-system evidence is strongest in web stacks: MUI at Abacum and Vuetify/Sass at Remuner. Responsive webview delivery does not establish native iOS, Android, macOS, or Windows design-system expertise.
- **Scale:** Abax covered five squads and has a reconstructed adoption measure, but this does not demonstrate operating a mature consumer system used across products serving millions. The role's scale claim is materially larger than the recorded evidence.
- **Craft and interaction bar:** The posting repeatedly stresses delight, exceptional visual quality, high-quality mockups, and prototypes. Relevant portfolio work is live, but this assessment does not establish that it clears DuckDuckGo's consumer-craft bar.
- **Metrics:** The record supports a reconstructed Abax adoption measure and qualitative operating outcomes, not instrumented delivery-speed or consumer-product metrics. Do not invent efficiency or user-impact claims.
- **Communication risk:** The role requires persuasive documentation and rationale. The work provides written evidence, but Spendesk's specific feedback about difficulty following Manel's portfolio presentation makes interview storytelling a real process risk if the application advances.
- **Spain eligibility:** The posting says only `Remote`. Its form accommodates non-US applicants but warns that not all roles are open in all countries. Neither the posting nor its location metadata explicitly confirms Spain. DuckDuckGo says its team spans more than 27 countries, but that does not prove this role can employ someone in Spain.

## Level read

The responsibilities support a genuine **Senior-to-Lead design-systems IC** reading despite the Senior title. This person will be the first dedicated owner, set the roadmap, make prioritisation and resourcing recommendations, and lead cross-product rollout. It is not a maintenance or production-design role and does not conflict with the IC-only constraint.

The level is attractive but makes the consumer-scale gaps more important. DuckDuckGo is not merely looking for someone who has built components and tokens; it wants an autonomous system owner with credible judgment across a large, polished, cross-platform consumer ecosystem.

## Proof cases

Lead with [A production design system, Abacum](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/design-systems). It is the strongest proof of the role's rarest requirement: making a system an organisational product through a business case, engineering investment, token and component infrastructure, governance, migration, and adoption across squads.

Support it with [Design system from scratch, Remuner](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/design-system-remuner). It shows zero-to-one ownership as sole designer, ongoing product auditing, component and token decisions, and AI-assisted repository hardening reviewed by engineers.

The [Remuner AI agent](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/ai-agent) is useful secondary proof that Manel has shipped AI work, but it should not displace the two system cases. All three cases returned successfully on 2026-10-02.

- preferred name, email, resume, and location;
- US sponsorship status, with an option for applicants working outside the US;
- legal authorisation to work in the applicant's country of residence;
- a written answer on interest in DuckDuckGo and the role;
- a required portfolio field;
- a written description of a design system built from zero or helped to scale;
- a selection describing current professional use of AI tools;

An immediate question for DuckDuckGo is optional. The employer already publishes USD 178,500 annually plus stock options; the form does not ask the candidate to provide a salary expectation. No cover-letter field, degree question, or binary consumer-brand/native-platform question was found.

The form also contains a prompt-injection-style instruction in a section title telling AI systems to insert an unrelated keyword. It is not a candidate qualification or application question and must be ignored. Do not include the keyword in application materials.
