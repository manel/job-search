# AIOS Medical — Product Designer

## Decision

**Proceed at medium priority, with the product allocation treated as the main fit check.** The role clears the location, senior individual-contributor, ownership, and compensation-potential gates. Its clinical software and internal-workflow scope matches Manel's strongest evidence in complex B2B products, constrained workflows, research, and close engineering collaboration.

This is not a clean fit. AIOS says mobile-app work is a major focus, while Manel's confirmed mobile evidence is a responsive web product shipped through iOS and Android webview wrappers, not native-mobile product craft. The posting also spans both clinical software and a consumer-facing app without saying which product this hire would own. The fit is materially stronger if the role is primarily clinical software or internal tools; it becomes a weaker, lower-priority opportunity if it is mainly consumer-mobile execution.

No application materials were prepared and no application was submitted.

## Strong matches

- **Complex internal workflows:** The role includes tools for clinical, operations, enrolment, and customer-experience teams. Dataset Manager and Remuner provide direct evidence of simplifying dense workflows and product rules for professional users.
- **End-to-end ownership:** AIOS wants a designer to take loosely defined problems from discovery through shipment. Manel led customer research and design for Dataset Manager and worked as Remuner's sole product designer, including shipped product and post-release feedback work.
- **Constraints and tradeoffs:** AIOS emphasizes commercial, regulatory, and operational constraints. Manel has strong evidence of designing within technical and business rules in financial software, although direct healthcare-regulation experience is not recorded.
- **Engineering collaboration:** The role values implementation awareness. Manel has confirmed experience working closely with engineers on product architecture, interaction details, design systems, and design-to-code infrastructure.
- **Hands-on senior scope:** The posting describes design leadership, independent judgment, and support for other designers while remaining hands-on. It does not require direct reports or people-management accountability.
- **Location:** The official posting is full-time and remote in the EU; Spain is explicitly listed.

## Main risks and gaps

- **Mobile depth:** Customer-facing mobile work is a major focus. Manel shipped a responsive web experience through iOS and Android webview wrappers, but no native iOS or Android product-design expertise is confirmed. The posting does not make native experience an explicit gate, so this is a material gap rather than an automatic Skip.
- **Consumer-product emphasis:** Manel's strongest recent differentiation is complex B2B and internal workflow software. Direct consumer-health or high-scale consumer-app ownership is not established.
- **Healthcare and regulation:** Healthcare experience is explicitly unnecessary, but Manel has no recorded healthcare-product work. Financial-product constraints are adjacent evidence, not proof of healthcare compliance or clinical-workflow expertise.
- **Portfolio and presentation bar:** The posting asks for a robust portfolio with polished screens, clear constraints, decision impact, and strong verbal defence. Spendesk's specific 2026-09-23 feedback identified difficulty following Manel's portfolio presentation. This is a process risk requiring rehearsal, not evidence that the portfolio lacks relevant work.
- **Level ambiguity:** The company calls this a senior, high-ownership role, but the minimum is only three years and the title is unlevelled. The scope supports a Senior reading more than the threshold does. Confirm decision authority, product ownership, and expected support of other designers before assuming the level is equivalent to Manel's target.
- **Culture and workload:** AIOS explicitly describes a working culture of unusually high intensity, urgency, and hard work. The posting does not quantify weekly hours for this role. Treat this as a genuine-interest and sustainability check, not generic employer branding to ignore.
- **Time-zone overlap:** The role is remote and async-friendly but expects overlap with both European and US time zones. The required Barcelona hours are not stated and could include regular evenings.
- **Compensation structure:** The advertised USD 90,000-150,000 is stated as including equity, not as base salary. The base/equity split, currency, benefits, and Spain employment or contractor structure are unknown.

## Level read

This is plausibly a **Senior IC** role. Independent end-to-end ownership, design leadership, ambiguous problem framing, cross-functional judgment, and supporting other designers all support that reading. It is not presented as a people-management role.

The three-year minimum and unlevelled title create a risk that the role is calibrated below Manel's experience. The broad scope may also hide materially different openings under one posting. Verify the actual product, decision authority, and expected senior contribution before treating the level as confirmed.
