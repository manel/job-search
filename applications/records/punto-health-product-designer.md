# Punto Health — Product Designer (UX/UI & Visual Design)

## Decision

**Proceed, light application, low-medium priority.** Seed-stage digital-health startup (cognitive and senior care: PuntoTest speech biomarker, PuntoCare AI support platform). The designer owns UX/UI across both products and the Figma design system, works closely with engineers, and also covers onboarding guides, landing pages, presentations, sales materials and campaigns. The must-haves are met: product-design portfolio, Figma and design systems, engineering partnership, visual fundamentals, fluent Spanish and English.
