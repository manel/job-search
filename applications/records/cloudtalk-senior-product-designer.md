# CloudTalk — Senior Product Designer

## Decision

**Proceed, at high priority, subject to compensation and cooperation-model checks.** The role closely matches Manel's strongest evidence: complex B2B SaaS, two production design systems, autonomous product ownership, cross-functional work with Engineering and Product, and a shipped conversational AI product. It is a remote Senior IC role open to EU-based candidates, and CloudTalk lists Barcelona as an optional R&D hub.

This is a strong match, not a complete one. CloudTalk asks for an expansive design-system track record spanning web, desktop, and mobile. Manel has strong web-system evidence and shipped a responsive product through iOS and Android webview apps, but the current evidence does not establish a native desktop or native mobile design system. Do not overstate this as equivalent multi-platform system ownership.

## Strong matches

- **B2B SaaS and complex workflows:** Abacum and Remuner provide direct commercial evidence in finance and sales-compensation products with dense rules, configuration, permissions, data, and user-facing explanations.
- **Design-system creation and operation:** At Abacum, Manel co-led the business case and worked across five squads on tokens, components, governance, migration, and the Figma-to-code pipeline. At Remuner, he built the system from scratch as sole designer and ran its governance with Engineering.
- **AI product experience:** Manel designed and shipped a conversational AI agent for compensation questions to all Remuner customers. He owned conversation design, answer formats, trust patterns, and the design side of capability boundaries; scope decisions were shared with founders and Engineering.
- **Autonomy and cross-functional influence:** The record supports independent ownership, work directly with founders and engineers, formal design-system prioritisation, and cross-squad operating practices without claiming people management.
- **Research and prototyping:** Dataset Manager and the modeling-language work show customer testing, research synthesis, concept revision, and translation of technical logic into safer product workflows.
- **Location and IC scope:** Manel is based in Barcelona and has confirmed permission to work in Spain. The posting and form are open to EU-based candidates and contain no people-management requirement.

## Risks and gaps

- **Multi-platform design-system depth:** Direct native desktop and native mobile design-system ownership is not established. The closest mobile evidence is a responsive web product shipped through iOS and Android webview apps.
- **Visual and presentation bar:** The role asks for elegant experiences across three platform categories and has interviews with the CPTO, a 90-minute product challenge, and the team. Spendesk's specific feedback identified difficulty following Manel's portfolio presentation. Factorial's challenge feedback also makes it important to state the future product value before validation detail. These are process risks, not evidence that the role itself is a poor fit.
- **Compensation and cooperation model:** CloudTalk publishes no range. The form requires gross annual expectations and a free-text preferred cooperation option before any recruiter conversation. It does not say whether a Spain-based hire would be an employee, EOR hire, or contractor. Do not reuse an employee salary figure as contractor revenue.
- **Level calibration:** The responsibilities support Senior IC scope through autonomy, complex projects, stakeholder communication, and system maturity work. The 4+ years threshold is soft and could indicate a broader mid-to-senior pool, so compensation and decision authority should be checked early.
- **System mandate:** The posting clearly assigns design-system maintenance and maturity work, but it does not establish dedicated engineering capacity, roadmap ownership, current adoption, or a specialist design-system team.

## Level read

This reads as a genuine **Senior individual-contributor** role, not Staff or Lead. The work combines product design with design-system stewardship and requires influence across Engineering, Product, and leadership. No direct reports or team-management accountability are stated.

## Proof cases

Lead with [A production design system, Abacum](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/design-systems). It proves the rarest central requirement: building the business case, securing engineering time, defining token and component infrastructure, and operating governance and migration across five squads.

Support with [Design system from scratch, Remuner](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/design-system-remuner) for sole-designer ownership and ongoing system maturity, and [An AI agent for questions about pay](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/ai-agent) for shipped AI-product work, trust patterns, capability boundaries, and post-release iteration.
