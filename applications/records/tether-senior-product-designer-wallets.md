# Tether — Senior Product Designer (Wallets)

## Decision

**Proceed as a medium-priority stretch.** The role clears the location, level, individual-contributor, and general work-shape gates. Manel's complex financial-product experience is relevant to Tether's broad `wallets, crypto or fintech` experience requirement. On 2026-10-02, he confirmed practical experience using self-custodial wallets and partial familiarity with the required crypto concepts.

That is enough to support the posting's `working understanding` language, but only narrowly. Manel explicitly does not claim deep crypto knowledge, and no professional wallet or crypto product-design experience is recorded. The application must present self-custodial-wallet use as practical domain familiarity and Abacum/Remuner as adjacent financial-product experience, not combine them into a false claim of shipped wallet work.

No application materials were prepared and no application was submitted.

## Fit assessment

### Strong matches

- **Complex financial products:** Abacum and Remuner provide direct evidence of translating dense financial and technical logic into usable product workflows. Dataset Manager is especially relevant to Tether's plain-language requirement.
- **End-to-end delivery:** The role spans concepts, prototypes, launch, and iteration. Manel has confirmed research, prototyping, design, delivery, and post-release feedback experience across Abacum and Remuner.
- **Autonomy and collaboration:** Manel was the first designer on the Dataset Manager squad and sole designer at Remuner, working closely with Product, Engineering, founders, and customers. The Tether role reports to a Design Lead and states no people-management accountability.
- **Design systems:** Tether lists scaled design-system experience as a preference. Abacum and Remuner provide strong evidence across components, tokens, governance, migration, and engineering workflows.
- **Tools:** The record supports Figma, high-fidelity interaction design, HTML/CSS/JavaScript familiarity, a shipped AI product, and AI-assisted design-infrastructure work reviewed by engineers.
- **Practical domain familiarity:** Manel has used self-custodial wallets and understands some of the terminology in Tether's requirement. This supports a truthful working-knowledge claim with the limitations below.
- **Location:** The official vacancy is remote worldwide and explicitly lists Barcelona, Spain.

### Main risks and gaps

- **Crypto and wallet depth:** No shipped wallet or crypto product is recorded. Manel's confirmed knowledge is partial rather than deep, so a recruiter may still prefer candidates from a wallet, exchange, payments, or crypto product. Do not claim professional crypto-product experience.
- **Portfolio specificity:** No current case demonstrates wallet onboarding, key custody, sending and receiving assets, fee or chain selection, transaction states, or irreversible-error prevention. The strongest cases prove adjacent complexity, not the role's core domain.
- **Consumer and first-time-user context:** Manel's strongest recent evidence is B2B SaaS for professional users. Tether emphasizes first-time wallet users; emerging-market experience is preferred. Neither is established in the evidence register.
- **Visual and prototyping bar:** The posting asks for mastery of visual design, interaction design, and rapid high-fidelity interactive prototyping. Relevant work exists, but this assessment does not establish that the portfolio clears Tether's craft bar.
- **Communication risk:** The job requires clear presentations to leadership. Spendesk supplied specific feedback that Manel's portfolio presentation was difficult to follow. This is an interview-process risk, not evidence of poor role fit or a reason to reject the opportunity by itself.
- **Compensation and engagement model:** Tether publishes no range, but the form requires an annual salary expectation in USD. It does not explain whether a Spain-based hire would be an employee, EOR hire, or contractor. Do not convert the current employee salary floor directly into contractor revenue.

## Level read

This reads as a genuine **Senior individual-contributor** role. It requires 5+ years, end-to-end execution, strategic input, daily cross-functional work, leadership presentation, and quality ownership, while working with a Design Lead. It does not require direct reports.

The scope appears execution-heavy rather than Lead or Staff: the hire shapes direction and contributes to strategy but does not own the wallet portfolio, design function, or team.
