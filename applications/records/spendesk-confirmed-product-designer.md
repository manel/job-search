He sometimes felt lost in his own story during the presentation and also struggled to answer Clement's recruiter-screen prompts asking for a specific past situation. This supports prioritising spoken storytelling and behavioural-answer practice as well as deck simplification. It is not independent feedback from Clement about his comprehension.
- **Interview opening, recalled by Manel on 2026-09-23:** Cyrièle asked him to walk through the case and explicitly requested problem framing. Manel is certain about “problem framing”; he tentatively recalls the remaining stages as exploration, solution, and outcomes. Do not present that full sequence as her exact wording or a verified assessment rubric. The request at the opening is context, not proof that a specific stage caused the rejection.
- **Resource shared by Cyrièle:** [The Muse: STAR interview method](https://www.themuse.com/advice/star-interview-method), reviewed on 2026-09-23. The article structures experience answers around situation, task, action, and result and recommends concise context, clear personal contribution, and spoken practice.
- **Inference:** Narrative structure is a sensible first improvement target given the comprehension feedback and resource. The report does not isolate slide density, pace, delivery, language, or particular slides as causes.
- **Recommended next step, not completed:** Build a short spoken version of one case with the problem, personal responsibility, key decisions and reasons, and supported outcome. Rehearse with someone unfamiliar with the work and ask them to repeat those points back before expanding the deck.
