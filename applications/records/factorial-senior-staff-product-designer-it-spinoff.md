# Factorial — Senior / Staff Product Designer, IT spinoff

## Outcome

Rejected after three rounds on 2026-07-02. The process included a talent screen, a Product Design Director interview, and a two-hour live design challenge used partly to assess Senior versus Staff level.

Unlike the generic screening rejections elsewhere in the tracker, Factorial provided specific feedback. The panel wanted stronger impact thinking: an independent shift from validation and MVP framing toward a clear future product vision and market proposition. Marta Serrano said the discussion had “stayed close to a manual, minimal solution.”

## Strategic lesson

For zero-to-one live challenges, lead with the compelling future state and its value, then work backward to the smallest validated step. Do not present careful MVP scoping as the ceiling of the concept.

This does not mean abandoning validation or inventing an unconstrained vision. The failure was sequencing and altitude: the panel could not see the ambitious proposition before Manel narrowed the solution.

Treat this as feedback about one live challenge, not as a general judgment of Manel's seniority or career.
