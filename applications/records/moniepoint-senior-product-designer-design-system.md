## Fit assessment

### Strong matches

- **Design systems as product infrastructure:** The role owns component libraries, tokens, documentation, governance, adoption, and the connection between design and production code. This is core product infrastructure with explicit engineering partnership, not a side responsibility.
- **Production track record:** Manel built Remuner's component library and design system from scratch as sole designer. At Abacum, he co-led the business case and worked on token architecture, the component library, governance, and the engineering pipeline across five squads.
- **Design-to-code alignment:** Moniepoint explicitly wants familiarity with how components are built and consumed in repositories. Manel has confirmed experience with Token Studio and Style Dictionary pipelines, engineering-reviewed repository changes, CI and drift checks, smoke tests, and outputs for implementation. Do not claim sole technical implementation of the original pipeline.
- **Governance and adoption:** The Abacum case shows organisational prioritisation, contribution rules, release and migration planning, library governance, and adoption across five squads. This is unusually direct evidence for the role's success criteria.
- **Domain:** Moniepoint's banking, payments, credit, and business-management products fit Manel's financial-software and complex B2B background.
- **Location and scope:** The official vacancy explicitly includes remote Spain. It describes Senior IC ownership and no direct-report accountability.

### Main risks and gaps

- **Native cross-platform depth:** The role explicitly spans Web, React, Flutter, Android, and iOS. Manel's confirmed design-system evidence is strongest in web stacks: MUI at Abacum and Vuetify/Sass at Remuner. No confirmed production design-system work for native iOS, Android, or Flutter is recorded. This is the main fit gap and should be stated plainly if asked.
- **Accessibility evidence:** The role asks for WCAG and inclusive-design expertise. The evidence register does not currently contain a specific accessibility project, audit, standard, or outcome. General familiarity must not be presented as demonstrated leadership without additional evidence.
- **Interaction and motion:** Manel's cases demonstrate component states, variants, and interaction decisions, but not a dedicated motion system. The posting mentions state transitions rather than requiring specialist animation tooling, so this is a secondary gap.
- **Measured efficiency:** The role defines success partly as accelerated shipping. Abacum has a reconstructed adoption measure and concrete migration state, but no instrumented cycle-time improvement. Use the recorded adoption and governance outcomes; do not convert them into an unsupported speed metric.
- **Compensation:** No salary range is published. The role clears the work and level gates, but financial viability remains unverified. Ask for the range before stating a number when the process allows.
