## Strong matches

- **Design systems as infrastructure:** At Abacum, Manel co-led the business case and worked across five squads on token architecture, components, governance, migration, and the design-to-code pipeline. At Remuner, he built the system from scratch and operated it with Engineering.
- **AI product work:** Manel designed and shipped a conversational AI agent to all Remuner customers. He owned conversation design, answer formats, trust patterns, and the design side of capability boundaries; scope decisions were shared with founders and Engineering.
- **AI-assisted implementation:** Manel used Codex to add CI validation, drift detection, smoke tests, previews, and implementation outputs to the Remuner token repository through engineering-reviewed pull requests. This is direct evidence of working beyond static handoff, within its recorded ownership limits.
- **AI-assisted product prototyping:** At Remuner, Manel repeatedly built working prototypes through AI-assisted workflows. Users could interact with them for testing, although they were not connected to the production application.
- **Mentoring and teaching:** Manel confirmed sustained mentoring of other designers across his career. He has also taught UX, components, and WordPress themes, supporting OLX's expectation that the hire can explain methods and help others develop.
- **Cross-functional product ownership:** Dataset Manager, the modeling language, and Remuner's sole-designer scope support research, technical collaboration, ambiguity, and end-to-end delivery.
- **Location and contract:** The role is fully remote within Spain, offers a permanent contract, and has no direct-report responsibility. Manel is based in Barcelona and has confirmed permission to work in Spain.

## Main risks and gaps

- **Prototype evidence needs stronger presentation:** The AI-assisted prototypes are now confirmed, but the record does not identify the specific tools used for them, individual test findings, or where they are visible in the portfolio. The application should make at least one example concrete without presenting it as production code.
- **Mentoring detail is still thin:** Sustained mentoring and relevant teaching are confirmed. The number and seniority of mentees, mentoring structure, and individual development outcomes are not yet documented, so the application should avoid invented scale or results.
- **Code-component contribution is only partly supported:** Manel has component-library, token-pipeline, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and engineering-collaboration evidence. The record does not establish repeated personal implementation of production product components.
- **Impact and prototype portfolio bar:** OLX explicitly screens for shipped work, live prototypes, and measurable business impact. The portfolio has shipped work and credible qualitative or reconstructed operational signals, but limited instrumented product metrics. No live product prototype is confirmed in the evidence register.
- **Consumer-marketplace domain:** OLX's marketplace products are outside Manel's preferred complex B2B lane. The cross-product systems and AI-foundation remit reduces that mismatch, but genuine interest in the domain is not confirmed.
- **Recent OLX rejection:** OLX rejected Manel before interview for a Senior Design System Designer application on 2026-08-04 without specific feedback. That outcome does not identify a cause and is not an automatic bar. However, this application should show materially different fit through credible AI-native product practice, not simply reuse the previous design-system positioning.
- **Compensation:** OLX publishes an annual bonus of up to 15% but no salary range. Base-salary viability against Manel's approximate EUR 60,000 Senior floor is unknown.
