# Bending Spoons — UX/UI Designer

## Decision

**Skip.** On 2026-10-01, Manel confirmed that a few days or one week in Milan would be acceptable, but several months would not be. The posting expects most days in the Milan office during the first few months, regardless of the eventual work location. Spain's remote-work eligibility and the Madrid office do not override that requirement.

The product-design, AI-assisted workflow, research, engineering-collaboration, and compensation fit are credible, but they do not justify ignoring a confirmed location mismatch. Reconsider only if Bending Spoons removes or materially shortens the initial Milan requirement. If that happens, the remaining competitive gaps are repeated production frontend-feature ownership, instrumented funnel/retention evidence, and uncertainty about the assigned product and level.

No application materials were prepared and no application was submitted.

## Strong matches

- **End-to-end product design:** The role covers early concepts, polished UI/UX, validation, QA, release, and iteration. Manel has confirmed evidence across complex B2B workflows, customer testing, product architecture, design systems, and delivery with Product and Engineering.
- **AI-assisted work:** The role explicitly starts work with an LLM and uses AI for specifications, risk discovery, prototyping, and code. Manel has shipped a production conversational AI product and has confirmed Codex-assisted implementation work on a design-token pipeline, reviewed and merged by engineers.
- **Engineering-aware decisions:** Dataset Manager, Abacum's modeling language, and both design systems show close design-engineering collaboration and tradeoff work.
- **Research and validation:** Manel led customer testing for Dataset Manager and customer-call synthesis and competitor benchmarking for the modeling language. Remuner's manager-view work also includes post-release qualitative feedback analysis.
- **Compensation:** The published typical range of EUR 66,065-107,837 elsewhere in Europe clears the approximate EUR 60,000 Senior base floor. An actual offer point and contract type remain unknown. Discounted company equity is optional compensation allocation, not a confirmed equity grant on top of salary.

## Main risks and gaps

- **Initial Milan requirement:** The employer expects most days in its Milan office during the first few months. Travel and accommodation support does not remove the practical commitment. Manel confirmed that several months is not acceptable, so this gate fails.
- **Production frontend code:** The role expects designers to use Cursor and Claude Code to create interactive prototypes and production-ready frontend code. The evidence register supports code-adjacent design-system pipeline work, HTML/CSS/JavaScript familiarity, and engineer-reviewed merged changes. It does not establish repeated ownership of production product-feature code. Do not claim otherwise.
- **Measurement:** The role explicitly tracks funnels, retention, and adoption. Manel has qualitative customer feedback, operational signals, and a reconstructed design-system adoption measure, but no confirmed instrumented product-funnel or retention outcomes. This is a portfolio-screening risk.
- **Product and domain ambiguity:** The vacancy is company-wide and does not identify the assigned product, platform, customer type, or domain. Bending Spoons operates both consumer and business products. Fit with the preferred complex B2B lane therefore cannot be confirmed before team matching.
- **Level ambiguity:** The unlevelled title and seniority-agnostic process do not establish Senior, Lead, or Staff IC scope. The compensation range is attractive, but it does not prove decision authority or level.
- **Visual craft bar:** The posting emphasizes exceptional, polished UI and delight. Relevant work exists, but this assessment does not establish that the current portfolio clears Bending Spoons' visual-craft bar.
- **Process cost and culture:** The employer explicitly describes a demanding environment and says tests can take several hours. This is a real time cost, not a fit advantage. Apply only if the role and working model are genuinely attractive.
