# Wizeline — Senior Product Designer, Barcelona Hybrid

## Decision

**Proceed.** This is a strong specialist design-systems match. The core work—component architecture, Figma libraries and variables, tokens, documentation, governance, and design-to-code alignment—maps directly to Manel's confirmed Abacum and Remuner experience. Barcelona hybrid is compatible with the location constraint, the role is individual-contributor work, and the official application is open.

Do not treat this as an unqualified top-tier product-design match. The advertised work is narrower and more execution-heavy than Manel's broader product-architecture and strategic scope. Compensation, hybrid cadence, the authority of the design-systems team, and continuity beyond the named account are not published and need early verification.

## Fit assessment

### Strong matches

- **Design-system production work:** The role centers on building and refining components, maintaining Figma libraries, documenting usage, and supporting product-area extensions. Manel built Remuner's component library and design system from scratch and co-led Abacum's system work across five squads.
- **Tokens and Figma variables:** Wizeline explicitly requires design tokens, variables, theming, and maintainability at scale. The Abacum case shows a two-tier token architecture and a Figma Variables to Token Studio to Style Dictionary pipeline. The Remuner case shows a second token system adapted to Vuetify and Sass.
- **Design-to-code alignment:** The role asks for engineering partnership and lists CSS, React component APIs, Storybook, and system tooling as useful experience. Manel has confirmed work with engineers on MUI and Vuetify component systems, Storybook, repository workflows, CI validation, drift checks, smoke tests, and generated implementation outputs. Do not claim sole engineering implementation of the original pipelines.
- **Documentation and governance:** Abacum provides direct evidence of contribution guidelines, naming conventions, library review, migration planning, issue reporting, release planning, and a weekly library sync. Remuner adds component documentation, a recurring engineering governance cadence, and an ongoing audit.
- **Independent IC work:** Manel was Remuner's sole designer and independently drove the system's direction and priorities while coordinating with engineers. The vacancy has no people-management requirement.
- **Experience threshold and location:** The posting asks for four or more years of product-design experience. Current approved CV copy records 15 years. The exact role is full-time and titled Barcelona hybrid.
- **AI tooling:** AI tooling is only a nice-to-have. Manel has confirmed Codex-assisted repository work that was reviewed and merged by engineers, plus shipped AI-product experience.

### Risks and gaps

- **Accessibility proof:** WCAG knowledge is a must-have, but the evidence register does not contain a specific accessibility audit, standard, remediation project, or measured accessibility outcome. General familiarity must not be presented as demonstrated accessibility leadership.
- **Multi-brand depth:** The job repeatedly mentions theming and multi-brand use. Token architecture and scalable theming are supported; a shipped multi-brand or white-label system is not currently confirmed.
- **Narrower scope:** The day-to-day work emphasizes components, library maintenance, documentation, and craft precision. It does not establish ownership of design-system strategy, roadmap, adoption metrics, or organisation-wide influence. This could underuse Manel's broader product and systems experience.
- **Consultancy/account uncertainty:** The official Greenhouse metadata lists `OPIS Barcelona` as the account. This suggests a client-specific assignment, but the posting does not explain the client relationship, project duration, or what happens between assignments. Confirm rather than infer.
- **Compensation:** No salary range is published. Financial viability against the approximate EUR 60,000 Senior floor is unknown.
- **Hybrid cadence:** The title says Barcelona hybrid, but the required office frequency is not stated.
- **Portfolio submission:** The application form requests a CV and LinkedIn profile but has no dedicated portfolio field. The CV therefore needs direct links to the two relevant cases.

## Level read

The title and hands-on expectations support a Senior specialist IC reading, but the four-year threshold and task mix leave some down-level risk. The role appears credible for someone who wants to spend most of the week inside the system; it is less convincing if the expectation is Staff-like strategy or broad product ownership. Verify decision authority, roadmap ownership, and team shape in the first conversation.
