# Cast AI (Kimchi) — Senior Product Designer

## Decision

**Skip.** Jobgether labels the vacancy as Spain-based, but Cast AI's exact live posting says it is location-specific and accepts applications only from Bulgaria, Croatia, Estonia, Greece, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, and Ukraine. Spain is not included. The direct employer posting is the canonical eligibility source.

The work itself is a strong adjacent match: a Senior IC role in a complex B2B AI product, with end-to-end ownership, independent research, product strategy, technical collaboration, and AI-assisted code prototyping. That substantive fit does not override the explicit country restriction.

No materials were prepared and no application was submitted.

## Strong matches

- **Complex technical and data-heavy products:** Dataset Manager and Abacum's modeling-language work show product architecture, research, and close collaboration with Engineering around technical constraints. This is relevant adjacent evidence, though the users were finance teams rather than software engineers.
- **AI product work:** Manel designed and shipped Remuner's conversational AI agent. He owned conversation design, answer formats, trust patterns, and the design side of capability boundaries; scope decisions were shared with founders and Engineering.
- **AI-assisted prototyping and code-adjacent work:** At Remuner, Manel repeatedly created AI-assisted interactive prototypes for user testing. He also used Codex to add CI validation, drift detection, smoke tests, previews, and implementation outputs to the token repository; engineers reviewed and merged the work.
- **Research ownership:** Manel led design and customer testing for Dataset Manager and owned customer-call synthesis, competitor benchmarking, and the function audit for Abacum's modeling language.
- **Senior IC shape:** The posting expects autonomy, strategy contribution, strong written communication, and technical collaboration. It does not state people-management responsibility.

## Main risks and gaps

- **Location:** Spain is explicitly absent from Cast AI's eligible-country list for this exact vacancy. Jobgether's Spain label conflicts with the employer's posting and should not be treated as proof of eligibility.
- **Developer-tool audience:** No direct product-design experience for software engineers, platform teams, coding agents, IDEs, or cloud-infrastructure users is recorded. Technical B2B SaaS and design-to-code infrastructure are adjacent evidence, not direct developer-tool experience.
- **Production code ownership:** The evidence supports AI-assisted user-testable prototypes and engineer-reviewed token-pipeline changes. It does not establish repeated ownership of production product-feature code or regular work inside a production application codebase.
- **CLI and desktop products:** No direct CLI or desktop-application design evidence is recorded. These are nice-to-have criteria, not mandatory gates.
- **Measured outcomes:** The posting expects ownership through measured outcomes. Manel has qualitative customer feedback, an implementation-team estimate, and reconstructed design-system adoption evidence, but limited instrumented product metrics.
- **Process cost:** Cast AI lists a home assignment followed by a case-study workshop. That is a material time investment and further weakens the case for pursuing an ineligible listing.
- **Compensation and engagement model:** EUR 4,500-7,000 gross per month annualizes to EUR 54,000-84,000 if paid over 12 months. The lower end is below Manel's approximate EUR 60,000 Senior base floor, and the posting does not establish the contract type or pay-period structure.

## If eligibility changes

Treat this as a medium-high-priority application, not an automatic top-priority one. Lead with [Dataset Manager](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/dataset-manager) for technical, data-heavy workflow design and independent customer testing. Support it with [Remuner AI agent](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/ai-agent) for shipped AI-product judgment and [Remuner design system](https://www.manel-leiva.com/cases/design-system-remuner) for AI-assisted, engineering-reviewed implementation work.

Before investing in the take-home process, verify Spain eligibility directly with Cast AI, the employment or contracting model, salary placement, and how strongly the hiring team requires direct developer-tool experience.
