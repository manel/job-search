# Yuno — Lead Product Designer

## Decision

**Defer until the Remuner case contains verified mobile evidence.** The domain, work shape, level, IC scope, and location are strong matches. Yuno is hiring a remote Lead IC in Spain to design complex payment products end to end, work closely with Product and Engineering, test with customers, use performance data, and contribute to its design system. These requirements align closely with Manel's complex B2B financial products, research, autonomous ownership, systems work, and engineering collaboration.

The blocking issue is explicit: Yuno lists experience across iOS, Android, and web as a minimum qualification and requires a portfolio showing end-to-end work across web and mobile. Manel shipped a responsive web product through iOS and Android webview apps, but no native-mobile expertise is confirmed and the current live cases do not present mobile work. On 2026-10-02, Manel confirmed that he can add relevant screens and copy to the Remuner design-system case. The posting does not say the experience must be native, so this is a credible route to satisfying the requirement. Keep the role deferred until the section is published and verified; then reassess whether it demonstrates meaningful mobile product decisions rather than only showing screens.

No application materials were prepared and no application was submitted.

## Strong matches

- **Domain and product complexity:** Yuno builds payment infrastructure for enterprise merchants, banks, PSPs, and wallets. Manel's strongest evidence is adjacent rather than exact: complex financial products in FP&A and sales compensation, including dense rules, permissions, calculations, configuration, and data workflows.
- **End-to-end ownership:** Dataset Manager shows discovery, customer testing, product-architecture decisions, collaboration through delivery, and post-launch operational signals. At Remuner, Manel worked as sole designer across the product.
- **Lead IC scope:** The posting explicitly labels the role an individual contributor. It asks for autonomous design leadership and cross-functional influence, not direct reports or team-management accountability.
- **Research and validation:** Manel led customer testing for Dataset Manager and used pre- and post-release feedback to shape the Remuner AI agent and manager-view work.
- **Technical collaboration:** The record supports close work with engineers on product constraints, interaction details, design systems, and design-to-code infrastructure.
- **Design systems:** Yuno treats system contribution as part of the role. Manel has stronger evidence than required through the Abacum and Remuner production systems.
- **Location:** The official posting is remote and explicitly includes Spain.

## Risks and gaps

- **Mobile portfolio requirement:** This is the main gate. The current live cases show web-based B2B work and do not demonstrate end-to-end mobile design. Responsive-web delivery through webview apps is relevant but is not equivalent to native iOS and Android craft.
- **Direct payments experience:** The posting calls fintech or payments experience a strong plus, not a minimum. The application nevertheless asks whether the candidate has worked in the payments industry. The current evidence supports financial-software experience, not a truthful `yes` to direct payments-industry experience.
- **English confirmation:** The posting requires fluent English and the form asks for an advanced level. The repository establishes professional work in English and separately records that formal presentations in English are less fluent than Manel wants. It does not contain a user-confirmed categorical proficiency level, so the form answer should be confirmed before submission.
- **Metrics ownership:** Yuno expects designers to identify project metrics and own their success. Manel has credible evidence of defining targets, reviewing product feedback, and tracking adoption or operational signals, but several outcomes are qualitative or reconstructed rather than instrumented product metrics. Do not inflate them.
- **Compensation:** Yuno publishes no range. The form requires an expectation before recruiter contact, while Manel's Lead IC target and the Spain employment or contracting structure are unconfirmed.

## Level read

This reads as a genuine **Lead individual-contributor** role. Yuno explicitly labels it `Individual Contributor`, requires more than six years of experience, and expects autonomous end-to-end ownership, strategic judgment, stakeholder communication, multiple-project management, and design-system contribution. No people-management responsibility is stated.
