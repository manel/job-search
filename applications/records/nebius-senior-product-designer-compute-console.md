# Nebius — Senior Product Designer, Compute, Nebius Console

## Decision

**Proceed, high priority among new roles.** Senior IC on the console for Nebius's AI cloud (Nasdaq-listed, about 1,500 people, HQ Amsterdam). The work is complex B2B workflow design: resource creation, configuration, management, monitoring and troubleshooting, with end-to-end ownership, IA and interaction models, close engineering partnership and design-system contribution. That matches Manel's core position almost line for line. The gap is domain: no cloud, infrastructure or developer-tool product, and the users are engineers rather than finance or sales-ops teams. Cloud experience is a nice-to-have, not a requirement.
