# WhiteTech — Senior Product Designer

## Decision

**Proceed, at high priority, subject to compensation and contract-shape checks.** The role closely matches Manel's strongest evidence across complex B2B financial products, data-heavy workflows, end-to-end product ownership, design systems, tokens, and close engineering collaboration. It is remote across Europe and the application explicitly includes Spain. No people-management requirement appears.

The main uncertainties are commercial rather than capability-based. WhiteTech publishes no compensation band, requires a gross monthly EUR expectation before screening, and does not state whether a Spain-based hire would be an employee, EOR hire, or contractor. The application also requires a yes/no answer on Ukrainian or Russian. ## Strong matches

- **Complex B2B and data-heavy product work:** The role explicitly asks for multi-step flows, forms, dashboards, tables, states, errors, and edge cases. Dataset Manager and Remuner's compensation product provide direct commercial evidence in complex financial-software contexts.
- **End-to-end ownership:** WhiteTech wants independent ownership from problem definition through implementation review. Manel led design and customer testing for Dataset Manager and was Remuner's sole designer, working through delivery and design-system implementation with engineers.
- **Design systems and tokens:** The role gives substantial weight to component libraries, variants, patterns, tokens, documentation, dependencies, and governance. Manel co-led Abacum's system across five squads and built Remuner's system from scratch, including token architecture, component states, governance, and design-to-code pipelines.
- **Technical collaboration:** Both systems include direct work with engineers, Storybook or framework constraints, repository workflows, migration planning, and implementation review. Preserve shared ownership of pipeline implementation.
- **Research and validation:** Customer research and prototype testing are nice-to-haves. Dataset Manager includes direct customer testing and changes to the product direction based on what users could understand and operate safely.
- **AI-assisted workflows:** WhiteTech explicitly wants responsible use of AI for documentation, specifications, and data organisation. Manel has confirmed AI-assisted repository work reviewed by engineers and a shipped conversational AI product.
- **Location and IC scope:** Spain is an explicit country option, CET working hours fit Barcelona, and the responsibilities describe senior individual-contributor work rather than people management.

## Risks and gaps

- **Exact payments domain:** Manel has strong financial-software experience in FP&A and sales compensation, but the evidence register does not establish direct EMI, PSP, open-banking, card, KYC, or payment-processing product ownership. Fintech is a nice-to-have, so this is not a gate.
- **White-label and multi-brand systems:** WhiteTech sells white-label products and values systems spanning multiple products or platforms. Cross-squad system work is confirmed; a shipped multi-brand or white-label design system is not.
- **Accessibility evidence:** Accessibility is a nice-to-have. The evidence register contains no specific commercial accessibility audit, remediation programme, standard, or measured outcome.
- **Presentation risk:** The role requires presenting and justifying decisions, and the process includes a Lead UI/UX Designer portfolio review. Spendesk's specific 2026-09-23 feedback identified difficulty following Manel's portfolio presentation. This does not negate role fit, but it is a material process risk requiring focused case rehearsal before an interview.
- **System mandate is not fully proven:** The posting makes the design system an important part of the role and includes implementation review with developers, which supports a genuine infrastructure reading. It does not identify a dedicated design-system team, roadmap authority, engineering allocation, adoption model, or current system maturity. Confirm these in the first conversation.
- **Compensation and engagement model:** No range or employment entity is published. The required monthly figure creates anchoring risk, and financial viability against the approximate EUR 60,000 annual Senior floor is unknown. Clarify whether compensation is salary or contractor invoicing, how many payments the monthly figure assumes, local statutory leave and holidays, benefits, expenses, termination terms, and whether the advertised 19 PTO days are the complete leave allowance.
