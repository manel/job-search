## Decision

**Proceed, at medium priority, with an early level and compensation gate.** The domain and work are unusually close to Manel's strongest evidence: complex B2B financial software, accounting workflows, data-heavy web products, research, testing, and AI. ## Strong matches

- **Domain and product shape:** Kabilio is building accounting, tax, payments, planning, banking, invoicing, and bank-transaction products for accounting firms and SMEs. This closely matches Manel's Abacum FP&A and Remuner compensation-product experience.
- **Complex web workflows:** The role is primarily web-based and concerns a multi-product financial platform. Dataset Manager, Abacum's modeling layer, and Remuner's role-based compensation workflows are direct evidence for making complex business logic usable.
- **Research and validation:** The posting asks for research, benchmarking, user testing, and iteration. Manel led customer testing for Dataset Manager and owned customer-call synthesis and competitor benchmarking for Abacum's modeling language.
- **AI relevance:** Kabilio is beta-testing Kabi and asks for experience with AI design tools. Manel has shipped a production conversational AI agent and uses AI-assisted design and implementation tools. Product AI experience is stronger evidence than tool familiarity alone.
- **Front-end fluency:** CSS and JavaScript are a plus. Confirmed current CV evidence includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, front-end component libraries, and design-to-code collaboration.
- **Location and contract:** Three office days per week in Barcelona and an indefinite Spanish employment contract pass the stated location and employment-shape constraints.

## Main risks and gaps

- **Level ambiguity:** The responsibilities describe competent end-to-end UX/UI execution but do not establish roadmap ownership, product strategy, mentoring, cross-team influence, or systems-level authority. The four-year minimum is soft for Manel's experience. This could be a genuine Senior role in a small team, but the JD does not prove it.
- **Founder-led decision model:** The role works closely with the Co-CEO who leads Product and Tech. Manel's stated preference is meaningful involvement in defining problems and discussing options with Product and Engineering after experiencing limited design influence in a founder-led environment at Remuner. This is a risk to test, not evidence that Kabilio operates the same way.
- **Compensation:** “Competitive salary” is not a range. The form forces the candidate to state a range before Kabilio discloses its budget, which creates anchoring and screening risk. Financial viability against the approximate EUR 60,000 Senior floor is unknown. The watchlist's single EUR 60,000 engineering report is not evidence of this design role's budget.
- **Visual/UI emphasis:** The posting repeatedly stresses engaging, visually appealing, high-fidelity interface work. Manel has relevant UI and design-system evidence, but this assessment does not establish that the portfolio clears Kabilio's visual-craft bar.
- **Data-visualization tools:** The JD asks for experience with data-visualization tools, but the evidence register does not name a specific visualization tool or a dedicated visualization project. Financial-data product work is adjacent evidence, not a substitute for a tool claim.
- **Professional Spanish:** Manel confirmed professional spoken and written Spanish on 2026-10-01. This gate passes.
