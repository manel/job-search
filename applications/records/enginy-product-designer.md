## Fit assessment

### Strong matches

- **Domain:** AI-enabled B2B SaaS for sales teams overlaps directly with Remuner's sales-compensation product and its production conversational AI agent.
- **Work type:** Enginy asks for ambiguous, end-to-end product work across discovery, prototyping, shipping, measurement, dense data interfaces, and AI behavior. This matches Manel's strongest positioning in complex B2B workflows, product architecture, customer testing, and AI trust patterns.
- **Engineering collaboration:** Manel has confirmed evidence of working directly with engineers on product architecture and design systems. At Remuner, he used Codex to extend the token pipeline with CI, drift detection, smoke tests, W3C token output, an HTML preview, and JSON output; engineers reviewed and merged the changes.
- **Design systems:** The role treats its design system as infrastructure for faster shipping rather than a standalone artefact. That matches Manel's work building systems at Remuner and Abacum.
- **IC scope:** No direct-report accountability is stated. Helping a junior Product Designer grow through the work is compatible with the IC constraint.

### Main risks and gaps

- **Maker bar:** Enginy explicitly wants a designer who can build working product prototypes in HTML/CSS/JavaScript, React, or an equivalent stack, work in the codebase and PRs, and fix issues directly. Manel has credible code-adjacent and merged-pipeline evidence, plus current AI-assisted building practice, but the evidence register does not yet establish repeated ownership of production product-feature code. Do not overstate this.
- **Location:** The source copy labels the role in-person. The benefits provide 48 work-from-home days per year for Product and Engineering, which suggests limited flexibility rather than a standard hybrid schedule. The expected weekly cadence is unknown.
- **Compensation:** No band is published. Enginy's description of salary as competitive is not decision-useful and should not be treated as evidence that the role clears Manel's floor.
- **Outcome evidence:** Enginy asks candidates to show what happened after launch. Manel has valid operational and release evidence, but limited instrumented product metrics. The application must use the evidence boundaries in `evidence/experience-facts.md`.
- **Genuine interest:** The domain and working model appear aligned with Manel's stated targets, but Manel has not yet confirmed interest in primarily in-person work or outbound-sales software specifically.

## Level read

The Greenhouse title is **Product Designer**, while the job body says **Senior Product Designer**. The responsibilities support a Senior IC reading: autonomous ownership of a major product bet, direct work with founders and engineers, outcome accountability, quality leadership, and mentoring a junior designer. It is not a people-management role.

The title mismatch and unspecified experience bar make level calibration worth confirming early, but the scope does not read as mid-level.
