## Strategic rules

- Never optimize a CV or cover letter without first assessing fit. Writing first wastes time.
- When fit is marginal, say so. Don't dress it up.
- Salary: follow the decision rules in `context/salary.md`. Prefer roles with a published range, ask for the range before giving a number when the process allows, and record what was asked and stated in the role's record. Never share previous salary if asked.
- Warm referrals are higher leverage than cold applications. Check for a warm path before every cold apply (`context/outreach.md`).
- Silent applications get followed up on schedule, not when remembered. Overdue `next_action_on` dates in `applications/records/` outrank new applications.
- Don't add case studies or portfolio work that doesn't improve hiring outcomes. Motion as evidence is appropriate. Motion as decoration is not.
