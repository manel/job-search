Hello LearnWise team,

Your role asks for two things I have done at the same company: design an AI feature that shipped to real users, and build a design system from scratch. I have also taught UX and digital design, which is part of why a product for educators and students interests me.

At Remuner I was the only designer on a sales-compensation platform, and I designed its conversational AI agent, which shipped to all customers. My part was the conversation design, answer formats that switched between tables and lists depending on the question, how the agent turned down off-scope questions, and what managers could see compared with sales reps. Engineering defined which data the agent could reach; the founders, engineering and I decided together what it would do. In the same role I built the component library and token system from scratch, and used AI-assisted workflows to make prototypes that users clicked through in testing.

At Abacum I led design and customer testing for Dataset Manager, which replaced JSON configuration with guided steps for finance teams. The implementation team's read was that standard setup took about half the previous time. I also co-led the business case for the Abax design system with the head of design and ran its contribution process across five squads. I have mentored designers throughout my career, and I would want to keep running critiques and system reviews here.

The AI agent case is at manel-leiva.com/cases/ai-agent.
