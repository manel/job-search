## Cover letter (exact, from sent PDF)

Dear Hiring Team,

This role caught my attention because of the problem space. Helping employees be productive from day one, across onboarding, permissions, equipment, and support, are workflows that most companies struggle to handle. There's real room to get them right.

I've done this kind of work before. At Remuner I joined as the first and only designer with no existing practice, no components, no documented patterns. What I find most relevant about this role is that it's not just about shipping features. It's about setting the foundations: the patterns, the standards, the way design works inside a team that doesn't have that yet. That's work I find genuinely motivating, and I have direct experience doing it.

Story used: sole designer at Remuner, no processes/research/consistency. Likely submitted (tightened version from the chat, possibly trimmed by Manel):

At Remuner I joined as the sole designer with no existing practice. No processes, no research, no consistency in the Figma files. The product was live and the team was shipping, but design decisions were being made on instinct and gut feel, with no way to validate them until users pushed back.

The challenge wasn't just the workload. It was knowing where to start when everything needed attention at once. I mapped the gaps, brought them to the team and my manager with a clear picture of the risk, and we agreed on a parallel track: start lightweight research rituals while building the component library from scratch.

It took time to see the impact, but by the end we had a shared language for design decisions and a system engineers could work from without involving me in every small change.
