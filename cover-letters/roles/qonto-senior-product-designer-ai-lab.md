## "Tell us about one AI-related transformation project" field

At Remuner, a sales compensation platform, I designed the AI agent that shipped to all customers. Sales reps could ask about their commission data in plain language: ERP line items, aggregations by period, how their plan works, where to focus. Managers used it too, for their own plans and their team's numbers. I was the sole designer on the product. I owned the UX and the conversation design, scoped the agent's capabilities with the PM and engineers, and defined the trust patterns: which questions the agent takes on, and how it shows uncertainty instead of guessing.

The impact I can claim honestly is qualitative. Pre-release interviews were strong, reps described it as a genuine tool to improve their work, and the agent went from concept to every customer's hands. We shipped without usage instrumentation, and that is the first thing I would do differently: define the success measures before release, not after. The second came from post-release feedback. The same object was a "deal", a "contract" or an "item" depending on the customer, so I iterated on per-customer terminology, tone and answer formats after launch. Vocabulary research with each customer during design would have caught that earlier.
