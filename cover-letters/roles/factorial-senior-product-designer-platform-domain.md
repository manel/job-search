- "What has been the most difficult situation you've faced as Product Designer? How did you handle it, and why was it challenging?" Answer as submitted 2026-07-14 (Story A, the killed-SQL angle):

  At Abacum, as the first designer on the integrations squad, I was asked to improve how finance teams configured their data. The assumed answer was a better UI, and a SQL-based power feature was already half specced. Research sessions showed the real problem was vocabulary: finance teams thought in dataset terms and the product spoke its own language. I made the call to drop the SQL direction and extend the existing modeling syntax instead, then had to defend that to stakeholders who wanted the more powerful feature. It was challenging because the evidence pointed against a direction people were already invested in, and there was no clean precedent to lean on; I built the case from session recordings and walked the team through the reasoning. The result was the Dataset Manager, which let finance teams configure their data without engineering support.
