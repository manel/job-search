Hello WhiteTech team,

This role caught my attention because it combines two parts of product design I enjoy: making complex financial workflows understandable and building the systems that help teams ship them consistently.

At Abacum, I led design and customer testing for the Dataset Manager, replacing exposed JSON configuration with a guided interface for finance teams. Testing showed that users thought in dimensions, mappings, and column formats while the product spoke in technical data types. I translated that logic into safer workflows with validation and clearer feedback. I also helped build and maintain Abacum's design system across five product squads, working on its component library, token architecture, governance, and engineering pipeline.

More recently, I was the sole designer at Remuner, a B2B sales-compensation platform. I worked directly with founders and engineers across the product, designed and shipped an AI agent, and built and maintained the component library and token system. An ongoing product audit helped us define missing states, variants, and behaviors and decide when each improvement should ship.

WhiteTech's mix of multi-step product flows, data-heavy interfaces, responsive design, and design-system work is close to the problems I have been working on. I would be interested in helping make those experiences clear for customers while keeping shared patterns and implementation aligned across the platform.
