Hello AIOS team,

This role includes internal tools for clinical, ops and CX teams, where some flows can't change because the business or the regulator says so. My recent work has been that kind of product in finance software, where commission rules, payout periods and financial models were fixed and my job was to make them usable for the people working in them every day.

At Remuner I was the only designer and there was no product manager, so I took problems from vague feedback to a shipped fix myself. When sales reps said their dashboard wasn't intuitive, I interviewed the people who had said it. They were reading the commission shown as the amount due this period, while payouts were quarterly. I designed a plan-calculation explainer and a payout-timing indicator, and in retesting users understood what they had been missing. I also adapted the web product for mobile and designed the app wrapper that shipped on iOS and Android in 2024.

At Abacum I led design and customer testing for Dataset Manager, which replaced hand-edited JSON configuration with guided steps for finance teams. The implementation team's read was that standard configuration took about half the time it used to. That case is at manel-leiva.com/cases/dataset-manager.
