Hello SplitMetrics team,

This role asks for information architecture, complex data visualisation and agentic flows in one seat. I've worked on all three over the last four years, in B2B products where people make decisions from dense numbers, which sounds close to how Acquire users manage their Apple Ads spend.

At Remuner I was the only designer on a sales-compensation platform. I designed and shipped an AI agent that lets sales reps and managers ask about their commissions and plans. I owned the conversation design, the answer formats and the questions it refused, and shared the capability decisions with the founders and engineering. When reps called their dashboard unintuitive, I interviewed them and found they were reading commission as the amount due this period, when payouts were quarterly. I redesigned it around plan calculations and payout timing, and product analytics then showed fewer clicks to reach a metric.

At Abacum I led design and customer testing for Dataset Manager, which replaced hand-edited JSON configuration with guided steps for finance teams. I also co-led the business case for Abax, the design system used across five squads, and worked on its tokens, components and governance.

The AI agent case is at manel-leiva.com/cases/ai-agent.
