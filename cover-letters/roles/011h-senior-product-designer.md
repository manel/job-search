Hello 011h team,

Your posting describes bid comparison, contracting, site planning and cost control living in separate Excel files and PDFs, and the job of turning them into one platform. I've done that kind of move for finance and sales teams.

At Abacum I led design and customer testing for Dataset Manager, which replaced hand-edited JSON configuration with guided steps for finance teams. Early on we tested a direction where users wrote data transformations in SQL. They could write the same logic as Excel functions but not in SQL, so the team extended Abacum's modeling language and kept it close to Excel.

At Remuner I was the only designer on a sales-compensation platform, covering data configuration, compensation plans and payouts, approval workflows and administration. When reps called their dashboard unintuitive, I interviewed them and found they were reading commission as the amount due this period, when payouts were quarterly. I redesigned it around plan calculations and payout timing, and analytics then showed fewer clicks to reach a metric. I'd start the same way with your site managers and suppliers, on site.

The Dataset Manager case is at manel-leiva.com/cases/dataset-manager.
