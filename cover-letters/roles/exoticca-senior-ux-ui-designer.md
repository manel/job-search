Hello Exoticca team,

Most of this role is about the tools your operations and business teams use every day. My work has mainly been internal and configuration-heavy B2B tools for expert users, including back-office interfaces.

At Remuner I was the only designer on a sales-compensation platform, covering data configuration, approval workflows, administration and the dashboards reps and managers used. When reps called their dashboard unintuitive, I interviewed them and found they were reading commission as the amount due this period, when payouts were quarterly. I redesigned it around plan calculations and payout timing, and analytics then showed fewer clicks to reach a metric. I also built the component library, owned the token repo, and brought the colours in use to WCAG AA contrast.

At Abacum I led design and customer testing for Dataset Manager, which replaced hand-edited JSON configuration with guided steps for finance teams. For 11 years before Abacum I co-ran a design studio, where I designed CMS and back-office tools and built and deployed CMS-connected front ends in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

The Dataset Manager case is at manel-leiva.com/cases/dataset-manager.
