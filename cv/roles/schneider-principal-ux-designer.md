SUMMARY

EXPERIENCE

Remuner, Senior Product Designer
May 2024 to March 2026, Barcelona (Remote)

Sole designer for two years, with no existing infrastructure to join; built the discipline itself, not just the system, to prepare the ground for future hires.
Set up a weekly design review, biweekly refinements, and onboarding and practice documentation covering process, principles, and task management, alongside the component library and token pipeline (Token Studio to Style Dictionary to npm).
Ran a continuous audit of UI patterns across the product, deciding fix-now versus deferred through monthly meetings with front-end engineers; this cadence is what the CTO called going from a 2010 app to a 2020 app.
Designed and shipped an AI agent, a chat surface where sales reps and managers ask about their commission data, sales plans, and team info; released to all customers.

Abacum, Senior Product Designer
January 2022 to April 2024, Barcelona (Remote)

First designer on the integrations squad, then the engine squad; co-led the design system business case with the head of design and presented it to engineering leadership.
Ran a weekly design library sync across the whole design team; set explicit success criteria for the rollout (80 percent of new UI from Abax components, full deprecation of legacy ones) and tracked adoption by counting components in the codebase.
Built the Abax design system alongside product work, a two-tier token architecture and a Token Studio to Style Dictionary to npm pipeline, adopted across five squads.
Designed the Dataset Manager, a UI layer over JSON dataset configuration that let finance teams configure their data without engineering support.

Kings of Mambo, Co-founder, UX Design Lead
2010 to 2021, Barcelona

Co-founded and led a UX studio for 11 years, mentoring junior practitioners across end-to-end product design for clients in media, retail, finance, legal, and culture, including a legal document management system and design plus front-end work for KYMCO.
Built much of what I designed: full WordPress themes wired to the CMS, component libraries in Sketch mapped to front-end code, and by 2020 a shared front-end starter kit with a build pipeline and a CSS variables settings layer.
Taught UX and digital design at postgraduate level, including a course taking a design file to a working CMS-driven site.
