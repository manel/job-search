Professionally, I have used AI both as a product designer and in my own workflow. At Remuner, I designed and launched a conversational AI agent for sales-compensation questions. My work included defining its capability boundaries, how it explained calculations, and when it should refuse to answer. We tested formats with users before launch, then reviewed conversations and feedback weekly to improve clarity, terminology, and trust.

I also use AI tools to support technical design-system work. For example, I worked with Codex to harden a token pipeline with CI validation, build-drift checks, smoke tests, and a visual preview. I use AI to speed up exploration and implementation, but not as a substitute for product judgment or verification.

Personally, I use LLMs to set up, maintain, and scale my homelab and home network. They are useful for troubleshooting, planning changes, and turning an unfamiliar technical problem into something I can test safely. I also use them as a light personal coach and tracker, mainly to structure routines and reflect on progress.
