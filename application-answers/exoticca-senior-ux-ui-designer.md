Tell us about your experience working as a UX/UI Designer.

I've designed digital products for 15 years. Much of that work has been B2B tools that expert users open every day, close to what your operations and business teams use.

At Remuner I was the only designer on a sales-compensation platform, covering data configuration, approval workflows, administration and the dashboards reps and managers used. When reps called their dashboard unintuitive, I interviewed them and found they were reading commission as the amount due this period, when payouts were quarterly. I redesigned it around plan calculations and payout timing, and analytics then showed fewer clicks to reach a metric. I also built the component library, owned the token repo, and brought the colours in use to WCAG AA contrast.

At Abacum I led design and customer testing for Dataset Manager, which replaced hand-edited JSON configuration with guided steps for finance teams.

Before that I co-ran a design studio for 11 years, designing CMS, e-commerce and back-office interfaces and building and deploying their front ends in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. In Figma I take work from flows and prototypes to high-fidelity specs, and I review the implementation with the developers.
