At Remuner, we received feedback that the dashboard for sales reps wasn't intuitive enough. I didn't want to assume that meant we needed to redesign the interface.

I identified the users who had given that feedback and arranged interviews to understand where they were getting stuck.

The same complaint described different problems. One user didn't understand how their compensation plan was calculated. Another couldn't tell which metrics affected it. A third saw a commission amount but didn't know when they would receive it, because their payout covered a three-month period.

We compared those findings with our existing evidence and prioritised the problems by severity, focusing on changes that could help users quickly.

As the sole designer, I designed all of the changes. For example, I added access to a summary of the plan calculation, with a route to the full detail. For quarterly payouts, I designed a period selector and information about payment timing and when the period would close.

The changes shipped. In testing, users could understand the information they had previously been missing. That gave us qualitative evidence that the changes addressed those specific problems.

The important point was turning one vague complaint into separate, actionable problems, each with its own solution.
