[Impact / research changed direction]

At Abacum, I led design and customer testing for a tool that helped finance teams configure their own data.

Before that, they depended on implementation specialists and technical configuration files. The goal was to give them more independence.

Our early direction combined a simpler interface with SQL. But testing showed something important: users could explain the transformation they needed using Excel functions, yet they struggled to express it in SQL.

I took that finding to Product and Engineering. Together, we changed direction and extended the existing modeling language around concepts familiar to finance users.

The guided workflow shipped. Our recollection was that customers could reach a working configuration in their first session. The implementation team also felt standard setups took roughly half the previous time. That was team feedback, not an analytics measurement.
