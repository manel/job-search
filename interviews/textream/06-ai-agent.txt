At Remuner, I designed a conversational agent for questions about commissions and sales plans.

As the sole designer, I owned the interface, conversation design, answer formats, and design-side boundaries. Engineering owned the technical architecture, and we agreed the scope with the founders. There wasn't a PM on this project.

We started from the data Engineering could make available. I designed the experience around supported questions, refusals for questions outside that scope, and different contexts for reps and managers.

Reps could ask about their own plans. Managers could also ask about their teams. Answers used tables or lists when that made the information easier to understand.

The agent launched to all customers. Feedback was mixed, and we continued improving tone, terminology, and presentation. I don't have reliable adoption or accuracy metrics, so I wouldn't present the launch as proof that every answer was correct.
