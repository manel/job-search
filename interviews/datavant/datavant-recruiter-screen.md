## The main proof story: Dataset Manager

### 60-second version

"At Abacum, finance teams depended on the implementation team to configure datasets because the underlying system was a JSON editor. I led the design and customer testing for a guided interface over that configuration.

The research showed that the problem was not only missing UI. The system spoke in technical concepts such as data types and schema configuration, while finance users thought in dimensions, mappings, and date formats. We changed the abstraction and language, added guardrails, and kept the underlying flexibility where it mattered.

After rollout, the implementation team's read was that standard configuration took roughly half the previous time, and customers could reach a working configuration in their first session. That was a team recollection rather than instrumented analytics, so I would not present it as a measured reduction."

### If Aideen asks how it connects to Provider Console

"The domains are different, but the product problem is close: understand where users need internal help, identify what is design-solvable, expose the right information and controls, and add enough guidance and feedback for users to complete the work safely themselves."

### If asked about multi-product platform experience

"My closest example is Abacum rather than a direct equivalent of five merged products. The datasets and modeling layers had evolved with different language and interaction patterns, and Abax supported consistency across five squads. At Remuner, I was the sole designer across the full product surface and ran an ongoing audit of patterns, states, and components."

This is a partial match. Do not claim that Abacum or Remuner involved consolidating five independent products.

"I would be new to provider workflows, so I would need to learn the terminology, regulations, and operational constraints rather than pretend they transfer directly. What does transfer is how I learn a complex domain. At Abacum, I worked with customers, implementation teams, Product, and Engineering to understand both the user language and the underlying system. ### How do you work with internal teams as research proxies?

"I treat them as an important source, but not as a substitute for users when direct research is possible. For Dataset Manager, implementation and customer-facing teams helped identify repeated support needs, while direct customer testing exposed the mismatch between the system's language and the users' mental model. I would compare those sources rather than treating one as complete."

### How do you work with Engineering?

"I try to make the design and technical constraints visible early. On Dataset Manager, the existing configuration model and risk of breaking customer setups meant we had to evolve the product incrementally. On the design-system side, I worked with engineers on tokens, component behavior, migration, and governance. At Remuner, repository changes I made with AI assistance went through engineering review before merging."

### What are you looking for in your next role?

"A hands-on Senior IC role where I can understand a complex problem, shape the product direction, and stay involved through delivery and iteration. I work best close to Product and Engineering. I also like improving shared patterns when that helps the product scale, but I do not want a design-system-only role."

### Do you want to manage people?

"My target is the senior individual-contributor path. I am comfortable mentoring, facilitating decisions, and raising the quality bar, but I am not looking for direct-report responsibility."

### Why should we move you to the next stage?

"The central challenge in this role is not new to me: turning expert-mediated, complex workflows into self-service products while creating consistency across a wider system. Dataset Manager is direct evidence of the first part, and my design-system work across Abacum and Remuner supports the second. I would bring that experience while being honest about the healthcare knowledge I still need to build."
