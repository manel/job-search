# Wizeline: salary script for the recruiter call (David Ho)
# Prepared 2026-10-20. Context: Wizeline's Design Systems Designer (Index) posting publishes EUR 36-38K; likely the same OPIS account (inference). Manel's floor for this role: EUR 40K gross annual, full-time employment contract (stated 2026-10-20; private, never say it).

## If David asks for expectations first

Ask once:
"Before I give a number, could you share the range for this role? I saw the Design Systems Designer posting at 36 to 38K, and I'd like to understand how this Senior role is levelled against it."

If he insists:
"For a Senior role with this scope in Barcelona, I'm looking at around 50K gross annual."
(One number, not a range.)

If asked about current or previous salary:
"I'd rather not share previous compensation, but for this role I'm targeting around 50K based on the scope."

## If his range comes back

- 48K or above: good; say it works in principle, subject to contract type.
- 40-48K: "I can be flexible for the right role. What would the offer depend on?" Don't commit in the call.
- Below 40K, or 36-38K for the Senior role: "That's below what I can consider for Senior scope. Is there flexibility?" Don't commit.

## Ask before any number is agreed

- Employment type: Spanish employment contract with Wizeline, or freelance (autonomo)? Freelance at the same headline figure is worth roughly 25-30% less.
- Is the role tied to the OPIS account, and what happens if the account ends?
- Hybrid cadence (the Index posting says three office days).

## Never

- Mention needing income soon or any financial pressure.
- Share previous salary.
- State the 40K floor.
