At Abacum, I identified a problem that forced users to leave the task they were working on.

When configuring a dataset, they could assign dimensions to its columns. But if a dimension was missing, they had to leave the dataset, create it somewhere else, and then come back. Customers reported that this caused confusion and errors.

I proposed putting basic dimension creation and editing directly into the open dataset. Product and Engineering agreed to build it.

We kept advanced management in the separate area, so the first version addressed the immediate workflow problem without moving everything.

The change shipped, and customers reported faster configuration, fewer errors, and less confusion. We didn't have a measured baseline, so I would describe that as qualitative customer feedback.
