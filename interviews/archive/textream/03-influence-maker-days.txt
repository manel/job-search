I co-led the business case with the Head of Design. We showed examples of inconsistencies across the product and compared different ways to staff the work.

The team chose a focused Maker Days week. That meant more people stepping away from squad work, but for a shorter period.

My contribution included the business case and parts of the token and component-library work. Engineering built the original implementation pipeline.
