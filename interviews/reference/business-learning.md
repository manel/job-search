# Business fluency: free learning path

Raised by Manel 2026-11-19. Gap: linking design work to user and business value in business terms. Perk asked for "adoption to be tied to user and business value" (2026-11-18).

Limit: this is not a move to PM. In an answer, business stays one line and one signal. Lead with the user and the journey.

The [Accelerate Design PM Masterclass](https://www.acceleratedesign.com/courses/pm-masterclass-for-designers) ($849, Ryan Scott) is out of budget. Its 5 modules: opportunities, prioritisation, metrics and testing, stakeholders, AI. The free resources below cover the parts that interviews test.

Start with [business-terms.md](business-terms.md), which is already in the repo.

## Practice

Reading alone does not change interview answers. For each impact story in [stories.md](../stories.md), write one business line: the user change, then the business effect it supports. Do it during workstream A of the [interview fix plan](../../analysis/2026-11-18/interview-fix-plan.md).
