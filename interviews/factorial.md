## Clarify first

The invite says "Senior Product Designer." The application was for "Founding Product Designer — Factorial tech spinoff." Confirm which role this screen is for within the first few minutes — ask it plainly, not apologetically. The scope difference matters: founding implies sole designer at a new product, senior implies joining an existing team. These require different stories.

Also: what is the spinoff? Factorial is an HRIS platform. Their tech spinoff is an unknown quantity. One of the most valuable things to extract from this call is what the spinoff does, its stage, and the design team size.

## What Factorial most likely cares about (this round)

1. **Solo or near-solo design experience.** A founding designer role is not a team hire. They want evidence you've operated without infrastructure, built things from scratch, and shipped without a safety net. Remuner is the proof.

2. **Whether you can move fast.** Spinoff = early stage. They want confidence that you won't need 3 months of process before shipping something.

3. **Fit signal: why Factorial, why now.** Lucia will probe this. Have a real answer about the company (Barcelona-headquartered, HR domain, spinoff as a greenfield opportunity) or don't fake one.

## Likely questions → story map

**"Tell me about yourself / walk me through your background"**
Two-minute arc: Abacum (product + DS at scale, multiple squads) → Remuner (sole designer, built everything from scratch, shipped DS and AI product) → now looking for a founding-scope role where the work has structural complexity. Don't narrate every job. Get to Remuner fast.

**"Why did you leave Remuner?"** ← Priority zero. Prepare this before you get on the call.
Company decision. The operating model was founder-driven — product direction came from the top, design input didn't have real weight in those calls. Legitimate model for some startups, not the right fit. Zero bitterness, pivot to evidence: system shipped, CTO's unprompted before/after quote. Then stop. Full answer in `interviews/prep.md` priority zero section — memorize that version.

**"Why Factorial / why this role?"**()
Answer honestly. Signals that work: Barcelona company building something greenfield, founding scope means real influence over product direction not just execution, HR domain has complex workflow problems (payroll, compliance, multi-market). Don't say "I love your brand." If you don't have a real answer yet, spend 20 minutes on their product and public writing before Monday.

**"What does founding designer mean to you?"**
→ Story C. Building without infrastructure. Setting the system before the system is needed. Making decisions that are hard to undo (token architecture, component structure) correctly the first time because reversing them later is expensive.

**"What's your experience working without a design team?"**
→ Story C + Story B (Abax DS business case). At Remuner: sole designer. At Abacum: co-led the DS push with the head of design, but the actual build was largely solo. You've operated independently before.

**"What are you looking for in your next role?"**
Founding-scope problem, structural complexity, design with influence upstream of the spec. Not executing a decided roadmap. Not a management track.

**"What are your salary expectations?"**
→ **€82K is the floor.** The "Founding" scope justifies the number — a founding designer is broader responsibility than a senior IC on an existing team. If they ask for a range, give the number, not a range. If they mention their band first, open at the top of it.
See `context/salary.md` — "Factorial: €82K is the floor given the Founding scope. If they push back, the counter is the breadth of responsibility implied by the title."
