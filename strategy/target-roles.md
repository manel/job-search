# Target roles and constraints

## Target

**Current priority, confirmed by Manel 2026-10-11:** Secure paid work as unemployment benefits are ending and family income is the immediate concern. Accept non-ideal product domains, lower compensation than previously expected and temporary roles when transferable skills make the work credible. Preferred roles below remain preferences, not reasons to reject viable income opportunities. No new numeric salary floor has been set. Location/no-relocation and IC constraints remain unchanged unless Manel overrides them.

- Senior Product Designer
- Lead Product Designer, individual contributor
- Staff Product Designer
- Design Systems Designer or closely related IC roles

## Preferred environment

B2B SaaS, fintech, data-heavy platforms, or complex workflow software. Prefer Series A-C startups and established product companies.

## Constraints

- Remote roles or hybrid roles in Barcelona.
- No relocation.
- Individual-contributor scope, not people management.
- Prefer product and systems-design work over UI-production, growth-marketing, or consumer-mobile craft-led roles.

## Fit gate

Assess every role before drafting. Evaluate domain, IC scope, work type, location, genuine interest, and relevant stack signals. For design-system roles, verify that the system is treated as product infrastructure rather than a side project.

A weak domain fit alone is not a skip under the current income-first priority. Assess credible transferable skills and practical viability; distinguish limited domain experience from inability to do the work. Genuine willingness to do the role matters more than ideal product interest.

The title alone does not establish level. Treat an unlevelled title, a four-to-six-year experience bar, or ambiguous ownership language as a level risk. Clarify scope and compensation early, but do not block an otherwise credible application solely because the range or Senior calibration is unknown under the current priority.

Explicit people-management requirements are a skip. Mentoring, critique, facilitation, and cross-functional leadership are compatible with the IC target; direct reports and team-management accountability are not.

For AI and design-system roles, confirm that the matching portfolio case is live and link directly to it. Do not rely on a recruiter to discover the evidence from the homepage.
