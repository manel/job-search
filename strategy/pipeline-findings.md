The main observed constraint is top-of-funnel conversion. It is not yet possible to isolate whether the largest driver is targeting, CV framing, portfolio proof, applicant volume, location, or another screen factor.

## What the evidence supports

### Level must be assessed from scope, not title

Biorce advertised a Senior role but explicitly rejected Manel because the position was effectively mid-level relative to his experience. CloudPay's unlevelled role carried the same risk, although CloudPay did not confirm its reason.

Rule: treat soft experience bars, unlevelled titles, and ambiguous ownership as risks. Verify scope and compensation early. Do not invest heavily in a package for a role that may be calibrated below Senior.

### Live-challenge framing needs vision before validation

Factorial's earlier process is the only deep interview with detailed panel feedback. The panel wanted Manel to move independently from a manual MVP toward a compelling future product proposition.

Rule: in zero-to-one exercises, state the future value and product vision first, then show assumptions, validation, sequencing, and the smallest credible release. Validation remains part of the solution; it should not hide the ambition.

### Repeated applications to one company have diminishing value

Bitpanda produced three screen-level rejections without human contact, including two applications to the same design-system role.

Rule: remove Bitpanda from routine sourcing. More generally, reapply after repeated screen rejection only when the role is materially different, the evidence has changed, or a credible warm path exists.

### Portfolio evidence changed during the dataset

The AI-agent case and homepage signal were added on 2026-07-21. The expanded design-system cases were completed and merged by 2026-07-28. Most early applications therefore did not show the portfolio that exists now.

Rule: do not treat early screen outcomes as a clean evaluation of the current portfolio. Start a new prospective cohort using the current portfolio version. For AI or design-system roles, link directly to the matching case instead of relying on homepage discovery.

The historical design-system lane is the clearest weak segment: seven explicit design-system applications produced zero known human conversations. However, all seven were submitted before the expanded design-system cases were merged on 2026-07-28. This is a priority to retest, not proof that the current cases fail. Review the lane again after five well-matched design-system applications using direct case links and the current portfolio.

### Referrals help access but do not guarantee review

Biorce's referral converted to a recruiter screen. Perk's confirmed referral did not produce a hiring response. The sample is too small to calculate a reliable referral advantage.

Rule: pursue relevant warm paths, but do not treat a referral as pipeline progress until a recruiter or hiring-team member engages.

## What the evidence does not support

### Compensation pressure is a signal, not a demonstrated rejection cause

Rejected applications span stated or discussed positions from EUR 67,000-70,000 through EUR 81,000, while several generic rejections occurred where salary was not stated. No employer has explicitly identified compensation as the rejection reason. Biorce's explicit reason was a level mismatch. Murphy AI is the only case where Manel suspects salary contributed, but Murphy gave no reason.

There is nevertheless a repeated market signal. Manel recalls that, outside some published job-description ranges, recruiter conversations for Spain generally did not exceed EUR 60,000-65,000. Secfix specifically said that EUR 67,000-70,000 was somewhat high for Spain. This does not prove causation, but it does show that some target roles may have budgets below Manel's previous EUR 76,000-85,000 Senior positioning.

Do not collapse these two conclusions:

- **Causation:** the evidence does not show that salary caused the recorded rejections.
- **Market constraint:** recruiter conversations suggest that EUR 60,000-65,000 is a common ceiling in part of the Spain-based market, and higher expectations may reduce the available pool.

Use the employer's published band when available and ask for the range before stating a number whenever possible. For unbanded roles, compare the likely budget with Manel's explicit target and walk-away floor before investing heavily. Record the range given by the employer, whether salary was asked, the number stated, and the outcome. Reassess after a larger prospective sample.

As of 2026-09-15, Manel's working minimum for the right Senior Product Designer role is approximately EUR 60,000 gross annual base, with a preference to negotiate higher. This is a floor for deciding whether a role is viable, not the number to lead with automatically.

### Generic rejection emails cannot diagnose portfolio weaknesses

“Closer match,” “many qualified applicants,” and similar language are outcomes, not feedback. Do not convert them into claims about visual craft, seniority, location, AI depth, or design-system scale.

### Figma PDF exports may be an ATS-screening confound

**Confirmed observation, reported by Manel on 2026-09-15:** PDFs exported from the current Figma workflow contain outlined text rather than embedded fonts. This makes the files substantially larger and prevents the text from being reliably selected, searched, or parsed.

**Inference:** CVs exported this way may not be readable by an applicant tracking system and could be one contributor to low submitted-to-conversation conversion. The current evidence does not establish that these PDFs caused any rejection or quantify their effect.

Before submitting a CV, verify that its PDF contains selectable, extractable text and embedded fonts. Use an alternative export workflow when it does not. Record the PDF generation method for the next cohort so this factor can be separated from targeting, framing, portfolio proof, location, and other screening variables.

## Operating changes

1. Apply only after the domain, work type, IC scope, location, and genuine-interest gates pass.
2. Verify level before tailoring when the title and responsibility signals conflict.
3. Lead the CV and application with the proof for the role's rarest requirement.
4. Link the exact portfolio case for AI and design-system applications.
5. Seek a warm path before or alongside submission, without delaying a ready application indefinitely.
6. After repeated screen rejection at one company, require changed evidence, role shape, or access before reapplying.
7. Keep salary calibrated to verified level and published bands. Treat EUR 60,000-65,000 as an observed Spain-market signal, not a universal band or an automatic target.
8. Track application channel, warm path, portfolio version, outcome stage, and rejection-reason class prospectively.
9. Verify that every submitted CV PDF has selectable, extractable text and embedded fonts; record its generation method.

## Metrics for the next cohort

Review after the next 10 submitted applications:

- submitted to human conversation;
- human conversation to next round;
- direct versus warm-path conversion;
- conversion by role lane: product, AI product, and design systems;
- rejection stage and whether feedback was generic or specific;
- salary requested, salary stated, published band, and outcome;
- portfolio version and exact case linked.
- CV PDF generation method and whether its text was extractable.

Ten applications will still be a small sample. Use the review to identify large directional changes, not to claim statistical certainty.
