## Strongest alternative new Remuner story

**Compensation operations: closing, permissions and issue resolution.** The sources contain frozen/approved/closed states, role-specific flows, permission restrictions and contextual claims from items/metrics/payouts. This could demonstrate consequential workflow design and collaboration more strongly than a dashboard if Manel can recover a clear decision and delivery account.

Treat closing/access and issue handling as related candidates, not an assumed single project. Select one end-to-end scenario only if its actual relationship is confirmed. Minimum evidence: user problem, personal/shared responsibility, an important state/access decision, what shipped, and feedback or a limitation. Plan/payout configuration is another substantial candidate but needs the same attribution check.

## Make collaboration visible

At **Remuner**, include a short “design foundations” section alongside the platform story:

- Shared role/scope-based files, exploration/handoff separation and dated iterations, used by engineers, the wider team, Head of Product and later designer.
- Progressive disclosure and reduced cognitive load, illustrated by the IC dashboard rather than presented as abstract principles.
- Weekly CEO/CTO/CFO/product planning and ad hoc CTO/frontend refinement before handoff, with their confirmed purposes.

At **Abacum**, show the agenda and rotating host for design-team alignment, progress-dependent component reviews, and collaboration in research/system decisions. Name the actions and contributors rather than asserting “team player”. Meeting benefits, team-management responsibility and later-designer overlap are not established.

These practices support how Manel worked with others. They do not need a standalone process case unless a distinct decision and supported result emerge.

## Keep in the searchable inventory

Notification/email architecture, feedback collection, currency formatting, SSO/login, mapping dependencies, HR documents and remaining model/configuration iterations widen the remit and may supply examples later. Do not give each a standalone case by default.

The feedback-system archive is especially relevant to design foundations, but artifacts alone do not establish an operational research or feedback program. Notification templates do not establish lifecycle-marketing ownership. Currency variants are not two independent projects. Existing Navigation/Roles/Settings page-index matches are not additional workstreams.

## Remaining evidence work

For the first selected Remuner example, establish the exact iteration, before/after problem, Manel's contribution, collaborators and release/feedback. No further broad inventory pass is necessary for the supplied sources. Numeric metrics are optional; a credible delivered change, qualitative feedback or honest mixed result is enough to build a useful story.
