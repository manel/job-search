**Ownership, three limits:**
- The two oldest pages (`2011` and `2021 -> Mappings (Dimensions)`) predate Manel's start at Abacum (January 2022). Not his work.
- The file is shared with other designers. Critiques on the canvas come from Joel (designer), Julian (head of design) and Baha. The 2401 page has a section called "Previous explorations / María", which is another designer's work.
- Everything else from 2022 on is assumed Manel's, consistent with the 2026-11-09 confirmation for the Dataset Manager files. It hasn't been confirmed page by page.
