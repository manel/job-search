They are the earlier designer's work and the brief Manel started from (the same pattern as Pagos vd Devengos, which Manel confirmed is the other designer's), not his designs. Those stories define the original model: optional per-plan approval flow, simultaneous approvers named by email, an approved period never changes even if source data changes.
