# Remuner | HR Compliance

File: [TMFqslrpYHy3Fz7E6B7028](https://www.figma.com/design/TMFqslrpYHy3Fz7E6B7028). 21 pages (13 with content), about 1,400 top-level nodes, 5,300 image fills (mostly keyscreen thumbnails). One feature area, January to June 2025: sales letters, the legal documents that tell each employee how their commission plan works, generated from plan rules, sent for e-signature and tracked. Full listing: [catalog/remuner-hr-compliance--TMFqsl.md](catalog/remuner-hr-compliance--TMFqsl.md).

Indexed 2026-11-09 from a full extraction. Ownership: Manel's. The exploration pages carry task notes in Spanish ("Tarea 1" to "Tarea 5") that list the open problems for the June iteration.
