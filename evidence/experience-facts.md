## Shared claim discipline

- Separate direct outcomes, operational signals, recollections, and plausible business consequences.
- Never turn inferred ARR or CAC impact into a numerical claim without evidence.
- Record ownership precisely when an engineer, product leader, or leadership team shared a decision.
- Older CVs may contain superseded descriptions. Check the current approved copy before editing.
